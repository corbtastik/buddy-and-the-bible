> **Acts 1:9-11** Now when He had spoken these things, while they watched, He was taken up, and a cloud received Him out of their sight. And while they looked steadfastly toward heaven as He went up, behold, two men stood by them in white apparel, who also said, "Men of Galilee, why do you stand gazing up into heaven? This same Jesus, who was taken up from you into heaven, will so come in like manner as you saw Him go into heaven."

There are four fundamental truths that have shaped the future of all believers & are a foundation for our faith & hope. Three are quite familiar to all (1) the birth of Jesus, (2) Jesus dying for the sins of all mankind, (3) the resurrection from the dead. Not as familiar as being important like the first three, (4) the accencion of Jesus.

Why is the ascension of Jesus as important? (1) Because Jesus left earth to prepare a place for His disciples. Jesus wants us to share his glorious home with all who believe and obey Him. (2) Because Jesus had to leave so that the Helper could be sent, to guide and help the Apostles to proclaim, confirm and preserve the gospel of salvation. (3) Because Jesus had to leave so that He could come again. When Jesus comes again eternity will begin, judgment will occur and the righteous will receive their reward of eternal life, in the home prepared for us. Jesus now, sits at God's right hand ruling over His kingdom the church, protecting and providing for His people. And we should look forward to His return!

> **1 Thessalonians 4:16-17** For the Lord Himself will descend from heaven with a shout, with the voice of an archangel, and with the trumpet of God. And the dead in Christ will rise first. Then we who are alive and remain shall be caught up together with them in the clouds to meet the Lord in the air. And thus we shall always be with the Lord.
