> *Luke 12:8-9 Also I say to you, whoever confesses Me before men, him the Son of Man also will confess before the angels of God. But he who denies Me before men will be denied before the angels of God.*

> *1 Corinthians 15:58 Therefore, my beloved brethren, be steadfast, immovable, always abounding in the work of the Lord, knowing that your labor is not in vain in the Lord.*

When we struggle through life, working to overcome our weaknesses and the temptations Satan presents us, when we endeavor to make ourselves to be like Jesus in our thoughts, words and deeds. God will recognize us, confess our names before the heavenly host, & acknowledge us as His children, as His servants and reward us according to His promises. That reward awaits us in heaven. Who could want anything more than to recognized and accepted by our God and then spend eternity with Him?

May we all strive diligently to receive the reward promised and waiting for us. We may suffer the wounds of battle but don't let them discourage and distract us. Keep your eyes on Jesus, your faith in His promises and your heart devoted to being more like Him.

> *Colossians 3:23 And whatever you do, do it heartily, as to the Lord and not to men, knowing that from the Lord you will receive the reward of the inheritance; for you serve the Lord Christ.*
