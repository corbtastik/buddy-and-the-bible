---
> **Luke 14:25-27** "If anyone comes to Me and does not hate his father and mother, wife and children, brothers and sisters, yes, and his own life also, he cannot be My disciple. And whoever does not bear his cross and come after Me cannot be My disciple. **33b** whoever of you does not forsake all that he has cannot be My disciple.

Jesus is talking about becoming a disciple, a Christian and His message is that there is a cost to being a Christian. Specifically, here He says being a Christian very well could cost you the relationship you have with your father, or your mother, even your wife or children. He then includes the extreme, Jesus said that being a Christian could cost a person his/her life.

God wants us to love Him more than anything else on this earth. We must be willing to put Him first in all things. First before our family, first before our friends, first before our education, or our career, most certainly before our hobbies and recreation. The point Jesus makes is that God expects us to willingly & lovingly give up all for Him.

> "*I gave My life for thee, My precious blood I shed..,*
> *I gave I gave my life for thee, what hast thou giv'n for Me?*"
