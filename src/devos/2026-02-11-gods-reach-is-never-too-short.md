> *Isaiah 59:1-2 Behold, the LORD's hand is not shortened, That it cannot save; Nor His ear heavy, That it cannot hear. But your iniquities have separated you from your God; And your sins have hidden His face from you, So that He will not hear.*

The prophet Isaiah was condemning the Jews for their wickedness in their departure from God's law. But as with all God's warnings and condemnations. There is God's plea for man to repent. God is sure to punish the sins of man, but always and forever eager to forgive if the sinner will simply stop their wicked ways and turn back to following Him.

There are two things that we can think about here, that are important lessons. First of all, we should never think that God can't save me. Isaiah clearly tells us that God's reach is never too short. No matter how deep into sin we might fall. God is willing and able to forgive. I mean if He can & is willing to save Saul of Tarsus who later described himself as "the chief of sinners" He is willing to save anyone who turns to Him for mercy according to His will.

Second, it's not God's fault that we are sinners. Too many people tend to blame God for their mishaps and mistakes. But God is not in the business of turning people to sin. God's interest in man is for everyone to be saved.

> *2 Pet. 3:9 The Lord is not slack concerning His promise, as some count slackness, but is longsuffering toward us, not willing that any should perish but that all should come to repentance*

Sin is exclusively man's choice and because of that. When we all stand before God in judgment man will reap the harvest he has sown.
