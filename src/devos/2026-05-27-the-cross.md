> **1 Corinthians 1:18** For the message of the cross is foolishness to those who are perishing, but to us who are being saved it is the power of God.

> **Galatians 6:12** ..only that they may not suffer persecution for the cross of Christ.

> **Colossians 2:14** having wiped out the handwriting of requirements that was against us, which was contrary to us. And He has taken it out of the way, having nailed it to the cross

The New Testament places a great deal of emphasis on the "cross" of Christ. When Paul was writing about the cross, he wasn't speaking of the wooden implement of death used by the Romans. Paul was using a figure of speech in which a part stands for a whole or a whole for a part. I might say "I am going to Whataburger for a hamburger" when in fact I will have a drink and some fries to go along with that hamburger. But going for a hamburger stands for the whole meal. The N.T. writers did the same thing with the term "cross" it was used to represent many things that were part of God's plan of redemption. Think about this, the cross includes God's hatred for sin, but then also God's love for mankind which then leads to...man's obligation to obey God's will. All these things are represented by the cross.

> **Galatians 6:14** But God forbid that I should boast except in the cross of our Lord Jesus Christ, by whom the world has been crucified to me, and I to the world.
