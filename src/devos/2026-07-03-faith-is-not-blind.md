> **Hebrews 11:1** Now faith is the substance of things hoped for, the evidence of things not seen.

> **2 Corinthians 5:7** "For we walk by faith, not by sight."

The Christian measures life by unseen things. Unbelievers live for the things they can see, taste and feel. Faith is not blind. Faith trusts the path Jesus sets before it in the Scriptures. Faith takes God at his word and does everything he says! Christians are on a journey to heaven. The faith that guides us, rests securely upon the evidence of verifiable truth, not on one's emotions, someone else's emotion. Not on your opinions or someone else's opinions.

With that said, self-examination is critical to having and maintaining a relationship with God. Take a careful look at yourself every day by using God's word as your mirror. If you are not following God's instructions, start obeying God's commandments without delay. If you put off obeying God your heart becomes calloused, making it harder to choose what is right.

> **Psalm 119:59-60** "I thought about my ways and turned my feet to Your testimonies. I made haste and did not delay to keep Your commandments."
