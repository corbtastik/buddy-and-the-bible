> *Ecclesiastes 3:1 To everything there is a season, a time for every purpose under heaven.*

The book of Ecclesiastes reminds us about what we know and what we do not know. We know life on earth ("under the sun") is cyclical, monotonous and that it cannot satisfy every longing, purpose and potential of mankind -- "*vanity of vanities, all is vanity*". Life on earth is good -- for the purposes God has given it. We are wise to accept this basic point affirmed by Solomon "*there is a time for every purpose under heaven*". God has designed life "*under the sun*" so that man will turn to him. But we must not put our hope in this life; it is temporary and unpredictable. In our lifetime we are given the opportunity to trust the purposes of God and to put our faith in him: "*walk by faith, and not by sight*" then "*fear God and keep his commandments*" -- this completes life's purpose, because in those things God is glorified. And to glorify God is the very reason man was created.

> *Psalm 8:4-5 What is man that You are mindful of him, And the son of man that You visit him? For You have made him a little lower than the angels, And You have crowned him with glory and honor.*
