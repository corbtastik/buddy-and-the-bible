**From the Hospital Bed -- "Life lines"**

The first IV blood transfusion from human to human took place in 1818 by James Blundell and from the work of other doctors and scientist. Doctors today have the ability to inject blood, medicine, hydrating liquids and even nourishment into a patient for treatment and the saving of lives.

When you wake up from surgery you find yourself connected to clear plastic bags with plastic tubes, containing all the necessary medicines to keep you comfortable and on the way to healing. Those IVs are very often "life-lines" for the patient. Without them patients would have to suffer many injections, much needed blood could not be transferred into the patient to save life. So there I was there trying to count the tubes connected to me.

We live in a sin-sick world and as disciples of Jesus we are striving to avoid and overcome the temptations the devil constantly places in our path. How do we stay faithful, fruitful and optimistic? How do we survive spiritually in this world filled with sin? The answer is God has provided "life-lines" for His children. Life-lines that help keep us connected to the only source of strength capable of defeating the devil.

**The life-line of God's Word**

> **John 6:68** But Simon Peter answered Him, "Lord, to whom shall we go? You have the words of eternal life.

The message of the bible is a living message, not only does it provide spiritual life, it motivates, encourages, and guides our thinking and in turn our actions. Without diligent bible study spiritual life cannot and will not exist.

> **2 Timothy 2:15** Study to shew thyself approved unto God a workman that needeth not be ashamed, rightly dividing the word of truth.

**The life-line of Prayer**

> **Isaiah 38:5** "Go and tell Hezekiah, 'Thus says the LORD, the God of David your father: "I have heard your prayer, I have seen your tears; surely I will add to your days fifteen years.

> **Matthew 21:22** And whatever things you ask in prayer, believing, you will receive.

Conversation connects, there is great truth in that statement. The fact is the more we take the time to pray to God the closer we are drawn to Him. Sad as it is far too many Christians fail to take the time to have a good long conversation with our God and Savior. Prayers for strength, encouragement and direction all will be answered when asked in faith. Without prayer our spiritual life wanes until it is no longer.

> **1 Thess. 5:17** Pray without ceasing.

**The life-line of Worship**

> **Psalm 95:1-2** OH come, let us sing to the LORD! Let us shout joyfully to the Rock of our salvation. Let us come before His presence with thanksgiving; Let us shout joyfully to Him with psalms. (6) Oh come, let us worship and bow down; Let us kneel before the LORD our Maker.

Aside from the fact that our God is worthy, deserving and expects our adoration. What too many fail to grasp is that God designed the mechanics of worship for His children's benefit. To build us up and help us remain faithful living in a world surrounded by sin. Every act of worship offers edification, the strength to keep on keeping on. And when we "worship in spirit and in truth" we gain those benefits. When we fail to give God the glory, our spiritual life is pretty soon gone.
