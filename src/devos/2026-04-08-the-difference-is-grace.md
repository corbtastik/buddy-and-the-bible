> *1 Timothy 1:13-16 Although I was formerly a blasphemer, a persecutor, and an insolent man; but I obtained mercy because I did it ignorantly in unbelief. And the grace of our Lord was exceedingly abundant, with faith and love which are in Christ Jesus. This is a faithful saying and worthy of all acceptance, that Christ Jesus came into the world to save sinners, of whom I am chief. However, for this reason I obtained mercy, that in me first Jesus Christ might show all longsuffering, as a pattern to those who are going to believe on Him for everlasting life.*

When Paul wrote to young Timothy, he described the difference between the Paul then and the Paul now. Paul said the difference between his present condition as a servant of Jesus and his past condition as a persecutor of Jesus was God's grace. And so, it is with each and every Christian. The only thing that separates us from all who are not Christians is God's grace and the blood of Jesus. We've nothing to brag about, we should as Paul said, boast only in the cross of Jesus.

R.C. Sproul wrote: "Christians have nothing to be smug about; we are not righteous people trying to correct the unrighteous. As one preacher said, 'Evangelism is just one beggar telling another beggar where to find bread.' The chief difference between the believer and the unbeliever is forgiveness."

> **Galatians 6:14** But God forbid that I should boast except in the cross of our Lord Jesus Christ, by whom the world has been crucified to me, and I to the world.

> **2 Corinthians 10:17** But "he who glories, let him glory in the LORD."
