It is time that we take our eyes off of the confusing, misleading input our senses are receiving from the world outside, and concentrate on the truths we can trust to guide us safely to our destination. (copied)

> *2 Timothy 3:16-17 All Scripture is given by inspiration of God, and is profitable for doctrine, for reproof, for correction, for instruction in righteousness, that the man of God may be complete, thoroughly equipped for every good work.*

It's never a bad thing to begin the day with a reminder of where our guidance for the day should come from. We have been provided "*all things pertaining to life and godliness*" it up to us to seek the answer and make application where needed. Plan this day to know what is right and what is wrong. Then where we are wrong get it right and keep it right.
