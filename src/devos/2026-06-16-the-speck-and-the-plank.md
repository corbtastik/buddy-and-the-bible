> **Matthew 7:2-3** "For with what judgment you judge, you will be judged; and with the measure you use, it will be measured back to you. And why do you look at the speck in your brother's eye, but do not consider the plank in your own eye?

How easy it is to see the faults of others while REFUSING to see the faults in ourselves and what's surprising is that we hold in contempt those whom we deem sinful while considering ourselves righteous! What a shame -- this is exactly the problem the Pharisees had in Jesus' day. When the Jews brought the woman caught in sin to Jesus and demanded His judgment about her. He turned the lesson back on them and reminded them that they too were sinful creatures and had no right to be judgmental towards another sinner.

A lot of times hatred and bitterness drive this sinful attitude. And as long as we harbor ill feelings towards others, we will always be guilty of hypocrisy. They will always be the sinner and ourselves the saint. That's why Jesus taught the importance "yea verily" the necessity of forgiving others when they sin against us.

> **Matthew 6:14-15** "For if you forgive men their trespasses, your heavenly Father will also forgive you. But if you do not forgive men their trespasses, neither will your Father forgive your trespasses.
