> **Romans 12:1** I beseech you therefore, brethren, by the mercies of God, that you present your bodies a living sacrifice, holy, acceptable to God, which is your reasonable service

We have all seen or heard the advertisement THE MARINES ARE LOOKING FOR A FEW GOOD MEN. And the whole sense of their advertisement campaign is to convince those would be soldiers that the Marines is the best branch of the service because they only take the best men or women into their ranks, only the intelligent, strong and brave!

And our text from the book of Romans suggests that God is also looking for the best of the best. God doesn't want the half-hearted service of a non-committed individual. Nor is He looking for lip service, those who only talk about Him or claim allegiance to Him and His commands. He is on the other hand looking for those who will serve Him with all their heart!

> **Matthew 22:37-38** Jesus said to him, "'You shall love the LORD your God with all your heart, with all your soul, and with all your mind.' This is the first and great commandment.
