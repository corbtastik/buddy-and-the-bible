> **Isaiah 55:7-9** Let the wicked forsake his way, and the unrighteous man his thoughts; let him return to the LORD, and He will have mercy on him; And to our God, For He will abundantly pardon. For My thoughts are not your thoughts, Nor are your ways My ways," says the LORD. For as the heavens are higher than the earth, So are My ways higher than your ways, And My thoughts than your thoughts.

Throughout the Bible there are numerous accounts where men chose to do things their own way rather than God's way, at times believing that they were improving upon God's pattern. But over and over again God shows us the folly of trying to "fix" His word. He has thoroughly proven that there is no better way than His way and that if we want salvation in Him we must respect that. God makes this point very clear through the prophet Isaiah. Sadly, however, in spite of all that the Bible says concerning this, there are many today who still try to "fix" God's pattern. But if we are to truly benefit from the wisdom and power of His word we must learn not to follow that path. We must learn from the scriptures to trust in God and to obey His commandments.
