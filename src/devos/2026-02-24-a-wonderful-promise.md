> **Matthew 21:26** "And whatever things you ask in prayer, believing, you will receive."

What powerful words, what a wonderful promise, what a great responsibility. Prayer has been from the beginning and remains one of the most important parts of man's relationship with God. It is both a blessing and a responsibility. We have been blessed with the right to approach the Creator of all things and the one true and living God and talk to Him as a friend, as a child would speak to His Father. But our blessing is also a responsibility because our relationship with God cannot and will not survive the challenges and temptations of Satan if we do not take the time to pray "without ceasing." Our relationship with others will never be what god expects it to be if we do not spend time in prayer on behalf of our neighbors.

While we do not know how God will answer every prayer but we must believe He does and faith accepts His answers whatever they may be.
