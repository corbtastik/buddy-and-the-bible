> **Philippians 1:12** But I want you to know, brethren, that the things which happened to me have actually turned out for the furtherance of the gospel,

Have you ever noticed that Paul never complained? Not about his ill health, nor his hard life, the fact he was unjustly imprisoned or that so many of the brethren hated him. He was able to see good in every situation whether it was imprisonment, beatings, persecution, hardships even shipwrecks -- Paul saw that there was good in every situation. We, on the other hand, who live in the richest nation in the world complain about anything and everything; our jobs, our health, our bills, our family, the weather, our neighbors on and on the list is unending. And what a complaining attitude leads to is the inability to ever see any good, which leads to an attitude of ingratitude.

One of the premiere requirements of being a Child of God is being thankful. But gratitude or thankfulness never comes until we are able to see and appreciate all the good God has done for us. Even when the good comes wrapped in plain and sometimes painful wrappings.

> **Colossians 2:6-7** As you therefore have received Christ Jesus the Lord, so walk in Him, rooted and built up in Him and established in the faith, as you have been taught, abounding in it with thanksgiving.
