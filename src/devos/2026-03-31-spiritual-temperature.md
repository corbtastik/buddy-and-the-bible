> *Romans 12:11 Not lagging in diligence, fervent in spirit, serving the Lord;*

> *Success in any endeavor is joined closely to one's enthusiasm or zeal for that endeavor.*

Successfully achieving the work of a Christian and the goals God has set before us depends upon our enthusiasm or zeal for the faith. Paul said we are to be "*fervent in spirit*". We are to be "boiling over" for the Lord and His work. So how exactly do we "check our temperature" to know whether we are the heated properly? Honest personal examination should and does reveal to every one of us just how enthusiastic we are for the Lord. Whether or not we are eager to serve and sacrifice or if the Lord's business is a weight we are struggling to drag around in our lives. But so many of us are like the man suffering chest pains who stands with his fingers over his wrist, checking his own pulse and concluding "the heart is still beating" everything must be alright! We simply DO NOT look inwardly objectively.

One good temperature check is our attitude toward serving others as well as worshipping God. A good temperature check list would include: How eager are we to assemble with the saints to worship God? How often do you speak to the Lord? How much do you enjoy studying the bible collectively and individually? How eager are you to help others when they are in need of encouragement, comfort and the necessities of life? How much are you willing to "sacrifice SELF" to serve someone else? Just what is your spiritual temperature?

> *Titus 2:13-14 looking for the blessed hope and glorious appearing of our great God and Savior Jesus Christ, who gave Himself for us, that He might redeem us from every lawless deed and purify for Himself [His] own special people, zealous for good works.*
