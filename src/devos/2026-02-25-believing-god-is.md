> **Hebrews 11:6** But without faith *it is* impossible to please *Him,* for he who comes to God must believe that He is, and *that* He is a rewarder of those who diligently seek Him.

Believing God is, goes beyond the acceptance that there is a God. Belief in God demands that we believe in who and what God is, as well. Some like to say "*the God I believe in is*..." and then they go on to describe a God that loves and accepts homosexuality; or a God that doesn't demand change or obedience by those seeking salvation. Others may believe that God picks and chooses certain individuals to either be saved or lost. Believing "God is", means accepting the description of God given to man in the Bible. Any description of God that isn't the product of inspired revelation is idolatry.
