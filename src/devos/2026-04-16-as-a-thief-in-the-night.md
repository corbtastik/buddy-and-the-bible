> *2 Peter 3:10-11 But the day of the Lord will come as a thief in the night, in which the heavens will pass away with a great noise, and the elements will melt with fervent heat; both the earth and the works that are in it will be burned up. Therefore, since all these things will be dissolved, what manner of persons ought you to be in holy conduct and godliness,*

Peter is very direct in his warning about Jesus return, the end of creation and final judgment. Each of us should understand that the time of Jesus return is unknown. As He taught in many of His parables, the return of the Master is unknown and unexpected. The lesson Jesus teaches and Peter reminds us of is we must be prepared, with the proper attitude and manner of life. These include (1) Looking forward and longing for His return as John prayed "*come Lord Jesus.*" (2) Being diligent about spiritual matters of life, serving God by serving others. (3) Living our life "in peace" with God, that is available only by and through the blood of Jesus, which is applied to our lives by our obedience to His commands.

> *2 Peter 3:14 Therefore, beloved, looking forward to these things, be diligent to be found by Him in peace, without spot and blameless;*
