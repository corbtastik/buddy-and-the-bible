> **James 1:18-21** Of His own will He brought us forth by the word of truth, that we might be a kind of first fruits of His creatures. So then, my beloved brethren, let every man be swift to hear, slow to speak, slow to wrath; for the wrath of man does not produce the righteousness of God. Therefore lay aside all filthiness and overflow of wickedness, and receive with meekness the implanted word, which is able to save your souls.

When I was growing up my Papaw Williams tried and tried to help me along, sharing his wisdom and experiences through his hard life, so that I might have it a little easier as I grew up. BUT I didn't appreciate what he said until I had grown up and made all the mistakes he warned against when I was younger. He loved me enough to take the time to teach me, yet in my foolishness I didn't listen.

There's an old song my grandparent's generation enjoyed called, "Love and Marriage." The first line of the song went like this 'Love and marriage go together like a horse and carriage." That is the lesson James is teaching there are some things that go together with receiving and obeying the gospel; urgency, meekness and purity. Somethings prevent hearing and accepting the gospel as we should, anger, sinfulness and pride.

We need to be willing to listen to the wisdom of God, it will guide us through the trials and temptations of life right into the gates of heaven.
