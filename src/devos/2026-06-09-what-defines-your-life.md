> **Luke 12:34** "For where your treasure is, there your heart will be also.

If they were to place a token of our life in our hands after we die what would it be? What single object defines your life? Would they place a phone, iPad, fishing rod, or a copy of The Shooter's Bible? Some wives would put the TV remote in the hands of their husbands. Because the reality of life is this, others know where our loyalties in life lie. Others know if we are godly men and women or not because our life defines who we are and who we truly love!

> **Proverbs 20:11** Even a child is known by his deeds, whether what he does is pure and right.

The great thing about life is this we can change what we are, so that we will be remembered in a godly way. Just how do you want to be remembered, by man AND by God?
