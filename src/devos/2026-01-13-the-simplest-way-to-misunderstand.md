> **Matthew 13:15** For the hearts of this people have grown dull. *Their* ears are hard of hearing, and their eyes they have closed, lest they should see with *their* eyes and hear with *their* ears, lest they should understand with *their* hearts and turn, So that I should heal them.'

Let me ask you, "what is the simplest way to misunderstand another person?" May I suggest that the simplest way to misunderstand someone else is TO FAIL TO LISTEN TO THEM. When we fail to listen to others we make a critical miscalculation. We mistake our misunderstanding for understanding, we think we know what they are saying or what they are about when in fact we really do not!

The question now becomes what is the simplest way to misunderstand God? Again, I understand there are many different things that lead to misunderstanding God but the simplest way to misunderstand Him is to FAIL TO LISTEN TO HIM. We fail to listen to Him and we think we have God figured out and His system figured out. The Jews failed to listen to God's law, and they felt that they could worship God in any way they wanted and could include in their worship the gods of the nations around them. But God reminded them that they had MISUNDERSTOOD "*My thoughts are higher than your thoughts, and my ways higher than your ways!*" and because of their misunderstanding they needed to repent. Man makes the same mistake about God's laws today, we fail to listen to God and then we come to the false conclusion that we have God figured out and His law figured out as well and we go about doing what WE think is best. When it comes to worship, marriage, living holy lives, treatment of our neighbors. But especially in the subject of salvation, when we fail to listen to God man comes up with a means of attaining eternal life far different from that which God has chosen.
