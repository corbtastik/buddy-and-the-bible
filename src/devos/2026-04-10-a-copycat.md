> *2 Peter 1:3-4 As His divine power has given to us all things that pertain to life and godliness, through the knowledge of Him who called us by glory and virtue, by which have been given to us exceedingly great and precious promises, that through these you may be partakers of the divine nature, having escaped the corruption that is in the world through lust.*

Paul warns us "be not conformed to this world." There is however, a sense in which there's nothing wrong with being a copycat. Children copy their parents and learn how to walk, talk, and deal with certain life situations like their parents (important that parents watch what they say and do!) At work beginners learn by copying those who by reason of time or schooling understand the job. Even spiritually we look to the characters in the Bible and follow their lead to overcome and live faithfully. (Hebrews 12:1) But we need to be careful as we seek to be like others, and not let our desire to be like someone else lead us away from being like Christ.

Paul wrote "imitate me as I imitate Christ" and his point is that we all should work to be like Jesus in all we say and do. He is the pattern by which all men will be judged and so the closer we get to being like Him the better off we are. So instead of looking to our parents, or mate or our friends, be independent in our thinking only seeking to be like the Lord, this makes us a leader instead of a follower in all we do. As the poet wrote: *O to be like Thee blessed Redeemer; This is my constant longing and prayer. Gladly I'll forfeit all of earth treasures, Jesus, Thy perfect likeness to wear.*
