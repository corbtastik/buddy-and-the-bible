> **Matthew 15:7-8** Hypocrites! Well did Isaiah prophesy about you, saying: 'These people draw near to Me with their mouth, and honor Me with their lips, but their heart is far from Me.

Jesus quoting from the prophet Isaiah condemns the Jewish leaders for their hypocrisy. You say that you are God's people, you pray loud, you condemn sinners, but in fact your heart isn't loyal to God you're loyal to yourselves and your own traditions. Jesus words could in fact literally describe the plight of many who profess Christianity. We allow our mind to be filled with so many things we cannot or refuse to concentrate on what we need to be focusing on. For example in prayer, we cannot spend time praying for others, giving thanks for our many blessings or asking for God's guidance because we are so wrapped up in our own busy lives. We allow our work, families, recreation and trivial problems to push the important issues out of our thinking, and actions. Remember the words of Jesus.

> **Matthew 6:33-34** "But seek first the kingdom of God and His righteousness, and all these things shall be added to you. Therefore do not worry about tomorrow, for tomorrow will worry about its own things. Sufficient for the day is its own trouble.
