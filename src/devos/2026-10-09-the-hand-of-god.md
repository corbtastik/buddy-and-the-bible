> **Psalm 48:10** According to Your name, O God, so is Your praise to the ends of the earth; Your right hand is full of righteousness.

> **Deuteronomy 32:39** 'Now see that I, even I, am He, and there is no God besides Me; I kill and I make alive; I wound and I heal; nor is there any who can deliver from My hand.

We use our hands for the purpose of carrying things in fact the hand was the first form of measurement that man had -- a handful measured amounts of liquid or grain or sand etc., a handbreadth was a length of measurement. When someone has his hands full means that he has all he can deal with whether in terms of holding something or addressing another problem. The Psalmist uses a full hand to describe the righteous character of God, God is filled with righteousness -- there is no unrighteousness whatsoever in God's person or character.

We also use our hands to execute judgment on the wicked. Parents who love their children spank them when they disobey them and that spanking is a form of judgment. Used to when people broke the law they were punished by being flogged or whipped in public, not only was the punishment painful it was humiliating. Moses describes God's judgment against the wicked. His hand is described as how His judgment is rendered.

But the hand of God is not something should always be feared because it symbolizes also God's love for His people and all of mankind.

> Daddy's hands, soft and kind when I was cryin'
> Daddy's hands, hard as steel when I done wrong.
> Daddy's hands weren't always gentle,
> But I've come to understand,
> There was always love in daddy's hands.

Hands not only represent strength power and authority but love, tender care and compassion.
