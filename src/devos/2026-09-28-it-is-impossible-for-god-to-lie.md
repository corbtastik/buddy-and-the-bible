---
title: "It Is Impossible for God to Lie"
date: 2026-09-28
time: 6:36 AM
scripture: "Hebrews 6:4-6"
summary: "He couldn't swear on anything higher, so He swore on Himself."
---
> **Hebrews 6:4-6** For it is impossible for those who were once enlightened, and have tasted the heavenly gift, and have become partakers of the Holy Spirit, and have tasted the good word of God and the powers of the age to come, if they fall away, to renew them again to repentance, since they crucify again for themselves the Son of God, and put Him to an open shame.

The writer is trying to motivate his readers remain faithful to Jesus. He warns of the dangers of falling back into disobedience. But then encourages us by reminding us that the hope we have in Jesus Christ isn't a false hope that comes from the mind or mouth of man but it rests on the sure and certain promises of God.

> **Hebrews 6:17-18** Thus God, determining to show more abundantly to the heirs of promise the immutability of His counsel, confirmed it by an oath, that by two immutable things, in which it is impossible for God to lie, we might have strong consolation, who have fled for refuge to lay hold of the hope set before us.

Two things assure us of the certainty of our hope. The fact that God cannot lie, His word is truth. Man does lie and man believes the lies told him -- But God is above that, it is impossible for God to lie. But also, God swore an oath, and His oath was based upon Himself. His unchangeable righteous nature. He couldn't swear on anything higher to assure you and me of the validity of His promise He swore on Himself. Now with that assurance (with that hope) we can face life, all its problems all its temptations knowing that the reward God has promised will be waiting for us if we will only trust & obey Him.
