> **Romans 14:11-12** For it is written: "As I live, says the Lord, Every knee shall bow to Me, and every tongue shall confess to God." So, then each of us shall give account of himself to God.

> **Acts 4:12** "Nor is there salvation in any other, for there is no other name under heaven given among men by which we must be saved."

Sadly, there are people who live their lives thinking they will be excused from their sinful choices because it was someone else's fault.

We're not going to be able to put our lives "in someone else's name" in judgment. But there is a name by which the mistakes we make can be erased so that we can stand before God justified -- or innocent on that day. It is by and through the name of Jesus that we all will be saved. Salvation comes when we call upon his name and appeal to Jesus for mercy and forgiveness. And that appeal is made through our obedience to His commands. If we find ourselves wanting on judgment day it will not be anyone else's fault but our own. God has extended His mercy to all but only through the name of Jesus we can receive forgiveness and eternal life, but we must make the choice.
