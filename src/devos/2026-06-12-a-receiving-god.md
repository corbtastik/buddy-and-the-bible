> **Hebrews 12:15-16** Therefore by Him let us continually offer the sacrifice of praise to God, that is, the fruit of our lips, giving thanks to His name. But do not forget to do good and to share, for with such sacrifices God is well pleased.

Jesus said, "*it is more blessed to give than to receive.*" And when we think of giving and receiving, we look to God who is the *giver of all good things*. And He gives willingly and joyfully blessings to mankind. But how many of us think of God as one who receives? I'll bet there are not many, because we just don't think of God in those kinds of terms. But God is a *receiving* God just as much as He is a giving God. And what I mean by that is God receives from mankind those gifts we willingly and freely give to Him, even though when we give to Him we are only giving back what He has given us.

The Hebrew writer tells us that our worship and our service to God are sacrifices to Him. But then the good deeds we do for others are also sacrifices that God receives and accepts. God has given many great and wonderful blessings to all of us. If He has given, we should and must give back. And He receives those gifts and they are store up treasures for us in heaven, from which we will be able to draw from on judgment day!
