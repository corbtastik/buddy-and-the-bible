---
> **Matthew 19:41-44** Now as He drew near, He saw the city and wept over it, saying, "If you had known, even you, especially in this your day, the things that make for your peace! But now they are hidden from your eyes. For days will come upon you when your enemies will build an embankment around you, surround you and close you in on every side, and level you, and your children within you, to the ground; and they will not leave in you one stone upon another, because you did not know the time of your visitation."

We must understand that while Jesus speaks to the physical city of Jerusalem, He is not weeping for the bricks, stones and timbers that the city is constructed from, but for the people. He is foretelling of God's punishment on a nation that had turned from God over and over. But God used them to bring a Savior into the world and then used them to crucify that Savior for the salvation of all mankind. He would have continued to bless the nation, like He blesses any nation that follows His laws of justice and morality. But Israel refused to repent.

> **2 Peter 3:9** The Lord is not slack concerning His promise, as some count slackness, but is longsuffering toward us, not willing that any should perish but that all should come to repentance.

God wants every sinner to turn from loving themselves to loving Him. Loving Him enough that we are willing to do what He commands, instead of what we want. We need to ask ourselves often, "Will God weep over me?" because I refuse to change my life?
