> *Romans 10:17 So then faith comes by hearing, and hearing by the word of God.*

God's promises, God's warnings, God's instructions ALL are testimony in the great courtroom of life, and we are either convinced of their truthfulness based upon the evidence presented to us in God's word. Faith does not come from personal experience, nor from God's direct operation on each individual. Faith is not implanted into our minds miraculously. *Faith comes by hearing and hearing by the word of God*. Plain and simple the sole origin of faith is God's word (the Bible). And to have faith everyone must study for himself or herself the truth therein.

It is the faith that is the product of God's word that leads one to obey God's commands because pleasing God requires more than simply believing He exists.

> *Hebrews 11:6 But without faith it is impossible to please Him, for he who comes to God must believe that He is, and that He is a rewarder of those who diligently seek Him.*
