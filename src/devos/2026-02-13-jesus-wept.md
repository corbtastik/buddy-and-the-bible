> *John 11:35 Jesus wept.*

Before she passed away Joy's mother shared with her just how she and Joy's father met. She said her mother was working on post at Ft. Sam Houston visiting with a friend, when her father walked up to the gentleman and to him that he was wasting his time with Miss Gray because he was going to marry her. As Joy related the story to me she began to cry, tears of sentimentality. When our first grandchild was born, Joy and I stood together and shed tears of joy. Only recently tears of sorrow have been shed in our church family, because of the loss of a dear loved ones. People shed tears for many different reasons, regret, pain, joviality. The human emotions are stirred by many different kinds of events. Jesus, also shed tears at the graveside of Lazarus because of His compassion for those who were mourning.

> *Luke 19:41 Now as He drew near, He saw the city and wept over it.*

When Jesus wept over the city of Jerusalem He was weeping because of the sinfulness of those who had turned their backs on Him and His message of repentance. They had rejected His message, denied the power of His miracles and refused to accept that He was approved of God. Which tells us Jesus is saddened when we turn our backs on His love, His commands, and His church. God desires that all men come to repentance but far too many refuse His grace and continue to live as they want.

Is Jesus weeping because of you?
