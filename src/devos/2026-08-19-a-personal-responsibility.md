> **Hebrews 6:1** Therefore, leaving the discussion of the elementary principles of Christ, let us go on to perfection, not laying again the foundation of repentance from dead works and of faith toward God,

> **John 8:32** "And you shall know the truth, and the truth shall make you free."

Jesus said the truth makes us free, and that is being free from sin. Within the revealed truth God has instructed man in the way to have his sins forgiven. But it can set us free only in as much as we "know the truth." What we need to know is found in the scriptures. Now the problem is how to transmit the information revealed in the Bible to ourselves? In our day of many modern conveniences, most people look for a way that is simple and doesn't involve much effort. People want the information transmitted automatically like having a bible and holding it in their lap and automatically the truth is transmitted into the mind. Ridiculous, yes but it seems like some folks believe it will happen. The most popular and convenient way today is to allow someone else to do the studying and tell what the bible says. Religious podcasts abound today, and it's easy to tune in listen and accept what the speaker is saying. The danger is there are more false teachers doing podcasts than teachers of truth. And some of those teachers sound pretty good, they quote scripture after scripture which sounds like they know what they are talking about. But those who accept their blather are led down the "*broad way to destruction.*"

Understanding scriptures is a personal responsibility, each one must prayerfully study and gain Bible knowledge for themselves.

> **2 Timothy 2:15** Be diligent (study KJV) to present yourself approved to God, a worker who does not need to be ashamed, rightly dividing the word of truth.
