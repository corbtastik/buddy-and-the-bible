> **Luke 3:7-8** Then he said to the multitudes that came out to be baptized by him, "Brood of vipers! Who warned you to flee from the wrath to come? Therefore bear fruits worthy of repentance, and do not begin to say to yourselves, 'We have Abraham as our father.' For I say to you that God is able to raise up children to Abraham from these stones.

When John the Baptist was preaching and baptizing in the region of the Jordan river many were coming to be baptized 'unto repentance'. But when the Jewish leaders came to be baptized, John rebuked them and then instructed them regarding what they should do to show repentance. When John taught about repentance, he spoke of a change, a change of heart. And indeed, if we have a change of mind, we must then put that change into action! We must stop living for self and as John told the Jews begin living for God.

One writer put it this way: The sure test of the quality of any supposed change of heart will be found in its permanent effects. '*By their fruits you shall know them*' is as applicable to the right method of judging ourselves as of judging others. Whatever, therefore, may have been our inward experience, whatever joy or sorrow we may have felt, unless we bring forth fruits meet for repentance, our experience will profit us nothing. What the writer is saying is; (1) repentance is necessary & (2) repentance is something that can be seen, **by the one repenting** as well as those around him. If we repent of our sins people will see that change and more importantly God will see it as well.
