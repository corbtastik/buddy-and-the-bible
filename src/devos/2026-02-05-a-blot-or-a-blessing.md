> *Matthew 13:33 Another parable He spoke to them: "The kingdom of heaven is like leaven, which a woman took and hid in three measures of meal till it was all leavened."*

Leaven in small amounts causes change in large volumes. If you've ever witnessed Joy making cinnamon rolls you know of which I speak. Just a little bit goes a long, long way.

Jesus warns us that there was a negative side of the power of influence, when He warned of the "leaven of the Pharisees." And the power of the bad is just as effective as the power of the good. "*Wisdom is better that weapons of war; but one sinner destroys much good*" (Eccl. 9:18) Paul put it this way "*bad companions corrupts good morals.*"

The influence of Solomon's wives led him to idolatry. The influence of Jonadab led Amnon to rape his sister. The influence of Ahab got Jehoshaphat wounded in battle. You & I are susceptible to being influenced in a bad way too if we are not careful.

On the other hand, we can be a powerful influence for good on our family, to our fellow workers, students and neighbors.

We will be either a "blot or a blessing, a blank we will not be." Don't think that just because you are one person that you don't have much influence one way or the other. An engineer class did an experiment. They suspended a 500 lb. bar of steel and then for 30 minutes swung a bottle cork against it. In that length of time they had the bar swinging like a pendulum. Even though we are one person in a sea of people we are exerting the power of influence. The question is what kind of influence will you be? A good influence begins with knowing and obeying Jesus, what do you choose?
