> **Romans 6:23** For the wages of sin is death,

You and I have amassed an unthinkable debt due to our sins. A debt that is impossible for us to pay. There is no way we can undo the sin we have committed, no amount of good deeds, will cover up our mistake, no amount of money will erase the sin we have committed, not amount of strength can make God forget that we have offended Him. Only God can forgive and ONLY GOD can pay the debt we owe. We just do not have enough resources to remove the debt that we owe to God. God sent His Son, Jesus Christ, to pay the debt for our sin. Because of who God is and what He has done for us, you and I are "indebted" to God. We owe Him our love, our loyalty, and our very lives.

> **1 Peter 1:18-19** "Knowing that you were not redeemed with corruptible things, like silver or gold, from your aimless conduct received by tradition from your fathers, but with the precious blood of Christ, as of a lamb without blemish and without spot"
