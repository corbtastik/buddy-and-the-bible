> **Matthew 7:13-14** "Enter by the narrow gate. For the gate is wide and the way is easy that leads to destruction, and those who enter by it are many. For the gate is narrow and the way is hard that leads to life, and those who find it are few.

You know there is only one choice in life that each and every human being must make only one & that choice is which road am I going to take. Which way do I choose the broad & easy way or the narrow & difficult way? We may end up making that same choice several times during our lifetime but it's always the same single choice which road am I going to take. We make that choice when friends invite or encourage us to do something wrong. We make that choice when our fellow workers want us to join them in some activity that is sinful. We make that choice when our desires are aroused and the wicked thoughts are knocking on the door of our mind. Every time we are tempted to do something that is against God's law -- we choose between the narrow difficult road and the broad and easy road.

Why choose the difficult way? Because the reward for doing right is greater by far than any pleasure or fun we might experience in time. The question is can we sacrifice a little to receive a lot? We can, but will we?
