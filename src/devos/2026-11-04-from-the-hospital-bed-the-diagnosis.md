**From the Hospital Bed -- the diagnosis**

So after waiting several days the doctors come in and inform you what's wrong with you. Some information is good and sometimes the information isn't. But you finally know what the problem is and can move forward to a solution.

> **Mark 2:16** When Jesus heard it, He said to them, "Those who are well have no need of a physician, but those who are sick. I did not come to call the righteous, but sinners, to repentance."

The very purpose of Jesus coming was to make man aware of our spiritual sickness. He came to "call sinners, to repentance." Before man will respond to Jesus call we have to accept His diagnosis of our spiritual needs.

> **Romans 3:23** for all have sinned and fall short of the glory of God, (6:23) For the wages of sin is death, but the gift of God is eternal life in Christ Jesus our Lord.

It's not easy to think about oneself as a sinner and realize that we don't measure up or can't earn salvation on our own. But we must understand we are all in danger of facing the just measure of our wrongdoings unless God provides for us some way to escape the consequence of our own decisions.

This brings us to a more positive aspect of the subject of salvation and that is the mercy of God. God might hate the things we do, but He loves every one of us. And He is willing to go to amazing lengths to satisfy His sense of justice without killing us in the process.

> **2 Peter 3:9** The Lord is not slack concerning His promise, as some count slackness, but is longsuffering toward us, not willing that any should perish but that all should come to repentance.

But we must be very careful not to allow our appreciation of God's mercy to take away the proper respect for God's justice. The power of the blood of Christ is infinite, but it does not automatically cover every human who has ever lived. That means God has established a series of conditions for our redemption, and only when we show our love for Him by meeting those conditions -- will the blood of Jesus cleanse us from our sins. What we need to understand is that these conditions that save us are the most important actions we can ever take. And we both need to understand them ourselves and be able to teach them to others.
