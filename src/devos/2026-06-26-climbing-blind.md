> **Proverbs 4:10-13** Hear, my son, and receive my sayings, and the years of your life will be many. I have taught you in the way of wisdom; I have led you in right paths. When you walk, your steps will not be hindered, and when you run, you will not stumble. Take firm hold of instruction, do not let go; keep her, for she is your life.

On May 25, 2001 Erik Weihenmayer made it to the top of Mt. Everest. Over 90% of those who attempt that climb never finish and 165 people have died trying to make it. But Erik's success is exceptional because he is blind. How did he do it? By listening. He would listen to the sound of a bell on the backpack of the climber in front of him. He would listen to the instructions of his fellow climbers, and he would know which direction not to go. He would listen to the sound of his pick as he hit the ice to tell if it was strong enough to hold his weight. Thinking about it, listening was Erik's source of life by helping him avoid death!!

All of us are on a perilous journey through life. It too is an uphill challenge on a narrow, difficult ledge. One misstep to the right or the left could result in spiritual death. How do we succeed in avoiding such a terrible end? By listening to the directions and instructions of God. And when we think of it, we are climbing this mountain blind, except for the words of guidance and instruction that God gives - we are completely and totally dependent upon God for guidance, without His help we indeed will fall into the abyss of eternal destruction. So as Jesus says, "*He that has ears, let him HEAR.*"
