> **Leviticus 13:2** "When a man has on the skin of his body a swelling, a scab, or a bright spot, and it becomes on the skin of his body like a leprous sore, then he shall be brought to Aaron the priest or to one of his sons the priests.

The Disease of Leprosy is used as a type of sin throughout the bible and is similar in several ways. The disease begins with just a small white sore or swollen spot and if unchecked will eventually cover the entire body from head to toe. Sin too begins very small but if not checked will completely control every thought and every action a person has.

> **James 1:14-15** But each one is tempted when he is drawn away by his own desires and enticed. Then, when desire has been conceived, it gives birth to sin; and sin, when it is full grown, brings forth death.

We need to be warned here, because if we are not careful this can happen to you and me. If we are not diligent to recognize our sins and are willing to repent of them, soon they become so ingrained in our being, repenting of them would be hard to nearly impossible. And one small sin that we fail to acknowledge and repent of can lead to a complete separation from Christ.

Failing to attend assembly is as good an example as can be given. If we make an excuse not to assemble one Wednesday or Sunday eveningand we justify it instead of seeing it as wrong and asking for forgiveness and determining not to do it again. Forsaking the assembly would no longer be wrong in our mind. Soon our conscientious would be calloused over to the point correction and admonition bounce off like bullets off the chest of Superman.

> **Hebrews 6:4-6** For it is impossible for those who were once enlightened, and have tasted the heavenly gift, and have become partakers of the Holy Spirit, and have tasted the good word of God and the powers of the age to come, if they fall away, to renew them again to repentance, since they crucify again for themselves the Son of God, and put Him to an open shame.
