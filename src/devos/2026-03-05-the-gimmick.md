> **Romans 1:16,** "For I'm not ashamed of the gospel of Christ, for it is the power of God to salvation for everyone who believes, for the Jew first and also for the Greek."

> **2 Timothy 3:16-17** "All scripture is given by inspiration of God, and is profitable for doctrine, for reproof, for correction for instruction in righteousness, that the man of God may be complete, thoroughly furnished for every good work.".

We live in a time when the gimmick has become the lifeline to membership and monetary stability to most denominations. Entertainment has become more expensive, extravagant, and novel to catch the attention of prospective members and givers. The antiquated "Sunday meal" no longer is sufficient to "bring-em-in." Gimmicks have to have flair and a special attraction to those who give the most. If churches are located in rural areas, the gimmick may take on the cowboy atmosphere of Billy Bob's where the religious can mix their faith with their pleasure and still go home feeling like "me and the Lord got a good thing going!" Too many rely on the inventions of man to draw people into the building in the "name of Jesus" to convert them to their denomination or their congregation, when the gospel itself is sufficient to draw the sinner to Jesus. It is the business of the Christian to spread the gospel of Christ and not the social gospel.
