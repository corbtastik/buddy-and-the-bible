> **Hosea 4:6** My people are destroyed for lack of knowledge. Because you have rejected knowledge, I also will reject you from being priest for Me; Because you have forgotten the law of your God, I also will forget your children.

I believe God is has appalled when His people have as little knowledge of His will as often as they do! The Jews who lived under the reign of many good kings of the Southern kingdom, were ignorant of God's law and because of their ignorance had wondered away from its precepts and commands. And because of their disobedience God was going to judge them.

I'll just bet someone back then sat in his home at night reflecting of the condition of the nation and would ask himself, "*How did we get in this shape?*" And Hosea supplied the answer. The people rejected knowledge. When we put off studying God's word it's not long till we don't want to study, and we don't want to know what the Bible teaches. And that ignorance will end up being punished, just like it was punished when Israel, rejected knowledge.
