> **2 Corinthians 4:8** We are hard pressed on every side, yet not crushed; we are perplexed, but not in despair;

That we face the devil each and every day of our lives is only too evident by the condition of the world we live in. We are surrounded by sinfulness at work, in school, when we try to recreate. Sometimes even when we are at home with the doors closed, sin manages to get into our homes and into our family's lives. And what is worse is that Satan hopes that each and every one of us will be affected by all this sin to his advantage. Paul's words are as applicable today as they were 2000 years ago. Literally we are 'crowded in.' You probably have felt as I have sometimes that Satan is winning and we are not going to survive. As was Lot the righteous today are vexed or oppressed by the wickedness that permeates our society, our community and our nation. We sing a song from time to time that asks the question how shall the young secure their hearts and guard their lives from sin? And in view of the condition of the world the question is one that we should ask ourselves regularly!

> **James 4:7** Therefore submit to God. Resist the devil and he will flee from you.

Even though we are flooded with wickedness we are commanded to resist the devil so as not to be overcome by his temptations and drawn away from Jesus back into the world.

> **1 Corinthians 10:13** No temptation has overtaken you that is not common to man. God is faithful, and he will not let you be tempted beyond your ability, but with the temptation he will also provide the way of escape, that you may be able to endure it.
