> **Psalms 119:11** Your word I have hidden in my heart, That I might not sin against You.

I got to thinking about it and there is a similarity between skin cancer and sin. Skin cancer destroys our skin, sin destroys our soul. Skin cancer comes from over exposure to the sun, sin comes from overindulgence in the "lust of the flesh, lust of the eyes & the pride of life." Skin cancer results in trips to the doctor, sin can result in a one way trip into eternal torment. Skin cancer can be prevented by applying sun blocker, sin can be prevented when we apply God's sin blocker.

The choice is ours we can work to prevent skin cancer. We can if we love God and love those around us work to prevent sin. Only God's word has the power to heal our sinful lives and help us overcome sin from developing to the destruction of our soul. Applying God's word to our life requires, study, believing and doing all God commands.
