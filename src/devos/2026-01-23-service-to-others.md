> **Matthew 22:37-38** Jesus said to him, "'You shall love the LORD your God with all your heart, with all your soul, and with all your mind.' This is *the* first and great commandment. And *the* second *is* like it: 'You shall love your neighbor as yourself.'

> **Philippians 2:3-5** *Let* nothing *be done* through selfish ambition or conceit, but in lowliness of mind let each esteem others better than himself. Let each of you look out not only for his own interests, but also for the interests of others. Let this mind be in you which was also in Christ Jesus,

Jesus tells us in Matthew 25 that our eternal destiny will be determined in part by our *service to others*. Those who were rewarded were rewarded for feeding the hungry, caring for the stranger, visiting the sick and encouraging the persecuted. Because Jesus said "inasmuch as you did *it* to one of the least of these My brethren, you did *it* to Me.' To become a true "servant of others" we must follow Paul's "step by step program."

We must develop a genuine "love your neighbor as yourself" attitude. I mean we can't just render lip service to this command. It MUST be a part of our character because if it is not we will only serve others when it is convenient for us or only when we want to. To the disregard of other's needs.

We must develop a genuine "esteem others better than himself" attitude. This is the tough one because it means that we must genuinely put others before ourselves. When our brother needs to be served we must be willing to give up our time, resources or talents and serve him.

We must also develop a genuine sense of "consideration" for the needs and opinions of others. Consideration comes from looking outward rather than inward, unselfishness and true consideration for the wellbeing of others.
