> **Proverbs 29:23** A man's pride will bring him low, But the humble in spirit will retain honor.

He was a quiet man, not boisterous or loud, not a leader in the since that he drove people to action, nor did he even charismatically motivate the masses to like him or follow his cause. He simply went about his life in a humble manner. He was honest and forthright, faithful to his word and not arrogant. In a time when most believe that to be successful or influential one has to "toot his own horn" "step up and speak up". He proved them wrong and God right. Who has more influence, the loud spoken ambitious individual or the quite unassuming person that simply goes about living in a reserved humble sort of way? This person spoke loud and influenced many but not with a "look at me" attitude, nor with a "if you don't do it like I do it's wrong" kind of personality. He never tried to prove he was right, nor did he have to defend his decisions -- he was in the simplest terms a meek and gentle man.
