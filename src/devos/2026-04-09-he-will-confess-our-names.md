> *Luke 12:6-9 Are not five sparrows sold for two copper coins? And not one of them is forgotten before God. But the very hairs of your head are all numbered. Do not fear therefore; you are of more value than many sparrows. Also I say to you, whoever confesses Me before men, him the Son of Man also will confess before the angels of God. But he who denies Me before men will be denied before the angels of God.*

When we struggle through life, working to overcome our weaknesses and the temptations Satan presents us, when we endeavor to make ourselves to be like Jesus in our thoughts, words and deeds. God will recognize us, confess our names before the heavenly host, & acknowledge us as His children, as His servants and reward us according to His promises. That reward awaits us in heaven. Who could want anything more than to recognized and accepted by our God and then spend eternity with Him? May we all strive diligently to receive the reward promised and waiting for us. We may suffer the wounds of battle but don't let them discourage and distract us. Keep your eyes on Jesus, your faith in His promises and your heart devoted to being more like Him.
