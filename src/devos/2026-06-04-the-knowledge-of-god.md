> **Proverbs 2:1-5** My son, if you receive my words, And treasure my commands within you, so that you incline your ear to wisdom, and apply your heart to understanding; yes, if you cry out for discernment, and lift up your voice for understanding, if you seek her as silver, and search for her as for hidden treasures; then you will understand the fear of the LORD, and find the knowledge of God.

Someone has estimated that, if all of man's accumulated knowledge from the beginning of recorded history to 1845 were represented by one inch, what he learned from 1845 until 1945 would amount to three inches and what he learned from 1945 until 1975 would represent the height of the Washington Monument. Can you imagine the "height" of knowledge today? Observe, however, this prophetic description of mankind throughout time:

> **2 Timothy 3:7** always learning and never able to come to the knowledge of the truth.

We may know how to get to the moon and how to transplant a human heart to save a life. But far too often we turn our back on the knowledge that benefits us the most "*the knowledge of God.*" The wisdom God has revealed to mankind is knowledge of how to get to heaven, much more important than getting to the moon and back. His wisdom provides us with the means of exchanging our sinful self for a purified forgiven self, that is accepted and blessed by God.

So open your bible and *receive*, *treasure*, *hear*, *apply*, *pray for*, and *search for* the knowledge of God's word, so that you will "*understand the fear of the Lord.*"
