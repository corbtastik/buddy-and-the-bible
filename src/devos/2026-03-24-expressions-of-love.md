> *James 4:7-10 Therefore submit to God. Resist the devil and he will flee from you. Draw near to God and He will draw near to you. Cleanse your hands, you sinners; and purify your hearts, you double-minded. Lament and mourn and weep! Let your laughter be turned to mourning and your joy to gloom. Humble yourselves in the sight of the Lord, and He will lift you up.*

I have to admit that one of the greatest joys I have been blessed to experience in my life is family. When the kids were small at the end of the day I'd come home and they would climb up in my lap and hug and kiss me there wasn't much I loved more than that. Even now I treasure their expressions of love as adults, as well as the expressions of love I receive from my grandchildren. Just as earthly fathers enjoy expressions of love -- so does our Heavenly Father. And drawing near to God speaks of the love we have for our Creator. James tells us that we draw near to God when we cleanse and purify our lives. John puts it this way. "*For this is the love of God, that we keep His commandments.*"

To love our Heavenly Father means we want to be like Him, in deed and in thought. If He is righteous His children will want to be righteous as well, if He hates sin, His children will hate sin too. Because we love Him, we will be diligent, seeking to be like Him.
