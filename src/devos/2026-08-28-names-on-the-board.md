> **Luke 10:20** "Nevertheless do not rejoice in this that the spirits are subject to you, but rather rejoice because your names are written in heaven."

> **Exodus 32:32-33** "And the LORD said to Moses, 'Whoever has sinned against Me, I will blot him out of My book.'"

In one of my classes in elementary school the teacher made those who misbehaved stand up and write their name on the chalkboard. If you misbehaved again you had to stand up and put a check by your name, if you got three checks you had to follow the teacher to the principle and then "ouch." But the next day when you got to school all the names on the chalkboard were erased, there was a "clean slate." Two thoughts about that, first ever day you got a chance to keep your name off the board and second the students couldn't erase their own name.

The bible talks about a list of names that each of us should desire to be on, but entries and erasures are not made by man but by God. The bottom line is this "*The solid foundation of God stands, having this seal: 'The Lord knows those who are His..'*" (2 Tim. 2:19) God adds to the list and God erases from the list. But the entries and erasures depend upon me and you by the life we live. If we are living in harmony with God's will, our name will be found in the book of life on judgment day. If we ignore God's commands, it will not.
