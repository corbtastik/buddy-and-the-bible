> **Ecclesiastes 3:14** I know that whatever God does, it shall be forever. Nothing can be added to it, and nothing taken from it. God does it, that men should fear before Him.

Someone asked once what is the purpose of the bible and one of its purposes is to reveal to man His Creator. So, we have from the beginning of time until the end of time the great and mighty works our Creator has done for the benefit of His creation (man). How does this information help us? It should, but sadly seldom does, create awe, respect and fear in the heart of man. The secret of enjoying a life that is temporary and is filled with things that are temporary is knowing that "*Whatever God does, it shall be forever.*" When He makes a promise, it is sure and steadfast and when He gives a warning it pertains to matters that cannot be changed. It is upon the "unchanging" nature of His directions, that we rest our hope in life. Because without God there simply is no hope for anything good beyond the life we live under the sun. People who deny the sovereignty of God throw away the very nature of their being and consequently their happiness.

> **1 Corinthians 15:19** If in Christ we have hope in this life only, we are of all people most to be pitied.
