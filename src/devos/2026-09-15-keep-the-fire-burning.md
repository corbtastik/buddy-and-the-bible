---
title: "Keep the Fire Burning"
date: 2026-09-15
time: 7:20 AM
scripture: "Titus 2:13-14"
summary: "The priests had to keep the fire burning continually. So do we."
---
When the tabernacle was completed God sent fire from heaven to light the fire under the altar and it was up to the priests to keep that fire going from then on and it was that fire they were to use in lighting the lamps in the Holy Place, it was that fire that they were to use to burn the incense before the Lord and it was that fire they were to use to burn the sacrifices before the Lord. Now in order for the incense to burn continually and in order for the daily sacrifice to burn continually -- the Priest had to keep the fire burning continually as well.

Fire is used to describe the attitude that we are to have toward God and His service.

> **Titus 2:13-14** looking for the blessed hope and glorious appearing of our great God and Savior Jesus Christ, who gave Himself for us, that He might redeem us from every lawless deed and purify for Himself His own special people, zealous for good works.

Here Paul says that God's working through Christ was to create His own people, a people who would by their very nature be zealous in their work for Him. This word comes from the Greek word that means literally to be on fire; or to boil over. We are to have a burning spirit for the Lord and for His service. And for that to happen we must keep our fire burning.
