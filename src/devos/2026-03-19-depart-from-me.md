> *Matthew 7:21-23 "Not everyone who says to Me, 'Lord, Lord,' shall enter the kingdom of heaven, but he who does the will of My Father in heaven. "Many will say to Me in that day, 'Lord, Lord, have we not prophesied in Your name, cast out demons in Your name, and done many wonders in Your name?' And then I will declare to them, 'I never knew you; depart from Me, you who practice lawlessness!'*

> *Matthew 25:41 "Then He will also say to those on the left hand, 'Depart from Me, you cursed, into the everlasting fire prepared for the devil and his angels:*

In all of time or eternity the saddest words any individual will ever hear are these words from the Lord "depart from me." In judgment they will mean departure into everlasting punishment -- away from all that is good and away from any form of hope. What is important for you and I to understand is that eternal "departure" from God begins in time when man departs from God because of faithlessness and sin. Christians are traveling a course set by God that leads to heaven and eternal salvation and in the course of living the danger of drifting off course is real and threatening each and every one of us. And we can drift so far away that we completely disregard God's word and when that happens, we have no place in our heart for repentance and when we have no place for repentance there is no chance of forgiveness.

> *Hebrews 3:12 Beware, brethren, lest there be in any of you an evil heart of unbelief in departing from the living God;*
