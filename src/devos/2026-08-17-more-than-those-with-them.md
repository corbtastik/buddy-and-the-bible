> **2 Kings 6:16-17** So he answered, "Do not fear, for those who are with us are more than those who are with them." And Elisha prayed, and said, "LORD, I pray, open his eyes that he may see." Then the LORD opened the eyes of the young man, and he saw. And behold, the mountain was full of horses and chariots of fire all around Elisha.

The Syrians had surrounded Dothan the city where Elisha was staying. In the morning Elisha's servant fearfully told Elisha they were surrounded by a great army. But Elisha assures him "*Those who are with us are more than those who are with them..*"

We are surrounded by the enemy, the servants of Satan, those interested in following their own course of life instead of God's. Our nation and society are becoming increasingly corrupt and perverse. Added to this is the natural course of life, we find ourselves sick, in pain, in need of medical assistance. Everyone has problems either at home, work or at school, family problems, or church problems. We have problems paying our bills, there is never enough money on payday to pay the bills and buy the groceries and pay the taxes and it is easy for Christians to be somewhat troubled as was the servant of Elisha "*What shall we do?*"

All of this means that the words of Elisha to his servant have meaning and can offer encouragement to us today. Because our eyes are often closed to the power of God and His love for His children. And we fail to see that "*they that be with us are more than they that be with them.*" We need to pray that our eyes be opened so that we might see what the servant of Elisha saw.
