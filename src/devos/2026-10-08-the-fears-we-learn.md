---
title: "The Fears We Learn"
date: 2026-10-08
time: 10:02 AM
scripture: "2 Timothy 4:9-10"
summary: "We are born with two fears and spend the rest of our lives learning the others."
---
Doctors say that there are ONLY two fears that humans are born with the fear of noise and the fear of falling, all other phobias are learned. We spend the rest of our lives learning to be afraid of -- dying or living; the future or the past; of being sheltered or being insecure; growing older or staying young; of being a failure or of being too successful; of being alone or being around others; of responsibility or of having nothing to do. We are so afraid of so many things. Some fears we grow out of some we grow into.

Fear like pride leads to other sins. Fear keeps from obeying God's commands. Fear renders us useless to the Lord. Fear most often spreads to others and causes them to sin as well. It is our fear that Satan uses to his advantage and he addresses our fear with things like;

**PERSECUTION**

> **2 Timothy 4:9-10** Be diligent to come to me quickly; for Demas has forsaken me, having loved this present world, and has departed for Thessalonica---Crescens for Galatia, Titus for Dalmatia.

Poor Demas coward that he was, couldn't face the possibility of losing his life. Certainly, no one wants to die -- but FAITH helps us to understand that there if a far better place than this waiting for those willing to be faithful unto death.

**FAILURE**

> **Matthew 25:25** 'And I was afraid, and went and hid your talent in the ground. Look, there you have what is yours.'

The one talent man just couldn't face that he might fail, he might try some endeavor and not be successful at it. What he refused to understand is that God hated his cowardliness more than failure. BECAUSE we are afraid of failure we do not take advantage of opportunities to speak up to those who are not Christians or have fallen away.

**REJECTION**

> **Galatians 2:11-12** Now when Peter had come to Antioch, I withstood him to his face, because he was to be blamed; for before certain men came from James, he would eat with the Gentiles; but when they came, he withdrew and separated himself, fearing those who were of the circumcision.

Here is a classic example of the SUCCESS of peer pressure. Peter didn't want to be rejected by his fellow Jews. We don't want to be rejected by our FRIENDS, FAMILY OR ASSOCIATES at work or in the club or on the block. NOTICE Peter wasn't a teenager, rejection is problematic with the older as well as the younger.

We could also speak about anger, hatred, or the desire for pleasure all of these things Satan still uses to drag us away from God into his dominion and power.
