> **Psalm 91:4** He shall cover you with His feathers, and under His wings you shall take refuge; His truth shall be your shield and buckler.

> **Psalm 119:151** You are near, O LORD, and all Your commandments are truth.

In his book "Brave Men" Ernie Pyle writes about a conversation between Maj. General Lucian Truscott, Third division commander and one of his men; "Sir I took my little black dog with me in my arms, and I sure was scared standing in that assault boat. Finally, we hit the beach and as we piled out into the water, we were more scared than ever. Then we waded ashore and looked around and there right ahead of me was a white house just where you said it would be and after that I wasn't scared."

What was it about that house being right where his commander said it would be, helped that soldier and his comrades overcome their great fear? It was knowing that your commanders, those asking you to put it on the line, are telling the truth. which increased their chances of survival.

Christians depend on truth first of all to become a Christian, but then we depend on the truth of God's word to remain Christians, growing in our knowledge and understanding of the truth by which we will be judged. Not only that but truth protects us from losing our compassion and love and at the same time gives us courage, comfort and encouragement when tempted.

> **Psalm 145:18-19** The LORD is near to all who call upon Him, to all who call upon Him in truth. He will fulfill the desire of those who fear Him; He also will hear their cry and save them.
