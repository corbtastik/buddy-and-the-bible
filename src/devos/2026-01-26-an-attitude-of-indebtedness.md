> **Romans 5:12** Therefore, just as through one man sin entered the world, and death through sin, and thus death spread to all men, because all sinned--

> **1 John 4:9-10** In this the love of God was manifested toward us, that God has sent His only begotten Son into the world, that we might live through Him. In this is love, not that we loved God, but that He loved us and sent His Son *to be* the propitiation for our sins.

There is a scene in the movie "Saving Private Ryan" where an survivor of the D-day assault in WWII, is kneeling by a headstone remembering the Lieutenant who died saving his life. It was very clear the man was deeply moved and indebted to the one who gave him life by giving up his own life. The movie depicts a common attitude among many veteran's whose lives have been saved because of someone else's courage and love, they never forget the debt they owe that person(s).

Just what does it take to move you to study God's word, to pray regularly to our Creator, to sacrifice our time, energy and resources to help someone else? What moves you to get up and attend every assembly of God's people? John and Paul tell us the sacrifice Jesus made should create in our heart such an attitude of indebtedness that anything and everything God asks us to do we will do it gladly and with a heart filled with gratitude for His love. If you fail to give God your all it is clear you really don't appreciate what Jesus did for us.
