> *Matt. 17:5 While he was still speaking, behold, a bright cloud overshadowed them; and suddenly a voice came out of the cloud, saying, "This is My beloved Son, in whom I am well pleased. Hear Him!"*

Have you ever noticed that there are some folks that always want to control the conversation by never giving anyone else an opportunity to talk? I'm not sure that there isn't a bit of insecurity involved there -- or a lot of selfish pride over-riding common courtesy.

James tells us that it is only with great difficulty that a person controls his tongue and therefore what he says. *For every kind of beast and bird, of reptile and creature of the sea, is tamed and has been tamed by mankind. But no man can tame the tongue. It is an unruly evil, full of deadly poison.* So, the greatest form of prevention & thus control is to listen more than we speak.

> His words were slow,
> His words were few,
> And never formed to glisten.
> But he was a joy
> to all his friends
> You should have heard him listen.
> --*Anonymous*

Just who are you listening to - or are you listening at all?
