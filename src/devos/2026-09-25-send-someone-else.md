> **Exodus 4:13** But he said, "O my Lord, please send by the hand of whomever else You may send."

Moses had been called by God to return to Egypt, face Pharoah and deliver the Israelites out of bondage and lead them to the Promised land. Moses has tried other excuses trying to get out of the great responsibility of leadership and confrontation. "Who am I.., I'm slow of speech.., they won't believe me." Now he begs the Lord to "send someone else" anyone else but me. Moses was "content" with his current situation. He had a job, hard work but no threat of death, he had a family wife sons and a father-in-law that was a priest. He was "safe and secure" and wanted to stay that way. Please let someone else do the job!

It is sad but many members of the Lord's church have exactly the same attitude Moses had. Content to sit in the pew and let someone else take care of all the other responsibilities required to keep a congregation growing. Let someone else clean the building, let someone else open the doors and turn on the heater/AC, let someone else teach the bible classes, let someone else lead in worship, let someone else visit the sick or visitors, let someone else..,

You know something the Lord wasn't happy with Moses when he offered that excuse, verse 14 reads, "*So the anger of the LORD was kindled against Moses..,*" The Lord is not pleased with "lazy servants" and will not reward them, because "letting someone else" do the work is a sin, if you have the ability and opportunity.

> **James 4:17** Therefore, to him who knows to do good and does not do it, to him it is sin.
