> **1 Samuel 3:10** "Then the Lord came and stood and called as at the other times 'Samuel! Samuel!' And Samuel said, 'Speak, for Your servant is listening.'"

Twice during the night the Lord had called to Samuel and he thought Eli the High Priest was calling him. But following Eli's instruction the third time the Lord spoke to Samuel, he responded "*Speak, for Your servant is listening.*"

One educator said that too often we train our children not to listen, he related the story of a mother who asked her son when he was younger, "Why do you immediately do what your father tells you to do?" Her son replied "Mom it is because you say it over and over again. Dad says it only once."

James instructs us to "*be swift to hear*" but far too often we only listen to God speaking to us through His word, when it's convenient, easy, and pleasant to hear. When His word corrects and rebukes there is a tendency to mute the volume. In order for us to hear God's word clearly, we must (like Samuel) be prepared to hear and then determined to do whatever it is God instructs us to do.

> **James 1:21-22** Therefore lay aside all filthiness and overflow of wickedness, and receive with meekness the implanted word, which is able to save your souls. But be doers of the word, and not hearers only, deceiving yourselves.
