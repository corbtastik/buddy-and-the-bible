> **John 3:15-16** And as Moses lifted up the serpent in the wilderness, even so must the Son of Man be lifted up, that whoever believes in Him should not perish but have eternal life. For God so loved the world that He gave His only begotten Son, that whoever believes in Him should not perish but have everlasting life.

Years ago my son Brad shared this story with our family.

> Tonight after bible class Parker told me that he wanted to be Baptized, I told him that I would talk to him when we got home. After we got home me and Brandi were in the office and I called him in there to talk to him. I asked him why did he want to be baptized and with a soft voice, a quivering lip and a tear in his eye he said "because I love the Lord." I will never forget that moment... *(Brad Payne)*

At midnight one summer at camp, a young camper wanted to be baptized for the remission of her sins and when I asked her why, she tearfully said, "Because if I die tonight, I won't go to heaven."

The love of God is a powerful thing when we open our hearts to His word and come to a realization of just how much He loves us. It will move us to total devotion and dedication to serving Him for a lifetime. But it is a choice we have to make, God will not force us to love Him, He simply reveals to us His love.

> **1 John 5:3-4** For this is the love of God, that we keep His commandments. And His commandments are not burdensome. For whatever is born of God overcomes the world. And this is the victory that has overcome the world---our faith.
