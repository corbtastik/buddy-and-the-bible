> **Luke 12:22-26** Then He said to His disciples, "Therefore I say to you, do not worry about your life, what you will eat; nor about the body, what you will put on. Life is more than food, and the body *is more* than clothing. Consider the ravens, for they neither sow nor reap, which have neither storehouse nor barn; and God feeds them. Of how much more value are you than the birds? And which of you by worrying can add one cubit to his stature? If you then are not able to do *the* least, why are you anxious for the rest?

Earl Pitts is a fictional character performed by Gary Burbank (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gary_Burbank), a radio (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radio) personality from Cincinnati, Ohio (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cincinnati) in 1968. The 2nd longest syndicated radio segment behind Paul Harvey News & Comments. He ended his satirical comments with "wake up Umerica!"

Christians far too often limit themselves and are not living the abundant life they could be. We need to understand that as Christians there is nothing holding us back except us. We must wake up and live for God and enjoy being saved children of God. I recognize the fact that this is easier said than done, but when we can learn to put our faith in God and what His Word says, we can learn to accept what Jesus has just taught us here. We can grow to the point where we realize that worrying about things is not going to change what happens. The only thing worrying will do is make you weak and keep you from living the kind of abundant life that God wants you to have. It will also help if we put our focus on living for God and His kingdom because if we keep our minds occupied with that, we will have less time to worry. There are many things out of our control, but as Christians we can turn our concerns over to God and know that everything will work out in the end.

> **1 Peter 5:6** Therefore humble yourselves under the mighty hand of God, that He may exalt you in due time, casting all your care upon Him, for He cares for you.
