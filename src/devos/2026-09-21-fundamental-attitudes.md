> **Proverbs 3:5-6** Trust in the LORD with all your heart, and lean not on your own understanding; in all your ways acknowledge Him, and He shall direct your paths.

If we lack trust in God founded on a living faith and if we are not determined to do God's will, we will never succeed in our endeavors to live righteous and overcome temptation and trial. These are fundamental attitudes that lead a person to seek and serve God. We must have them before God will even consider helping us. But remember they are only the beginning, they are a foundation upon which we build and add other attitudes and attributes that help strengthen us.

> **Psalm 27:1** HE LORD is my light and my salvation; whom shall I fear? The LORD is the strength of my life; of whom shall I be afraid?

> **Psalm 31:24** Be of good courage, and He shall strengthen your heart, all you who hope in the LORD.
