> *Proverbs 16:25 There is a way that seems right to a man, but its end is the way of death.*

> *Proverbs 10:14 Wise people store up knowledge, But the mouth of the foolish [is] near destruction.*

Many people in the religious community and even sometimes among members of the church believe in a church, preacher, or doctrine, because they feel good about it, regardless of whether they have found evidence of truth. Why aren't our emotions and feelings a good guide religiously? Ask yourself this question. Are feelings a good guide to matter that do not pertain to religion? You don't choose a doctor because you feel good about him, you want someone qualified. Very few places in life outside of our religion do we allow our emotions to guide our decisions -- why then are we so quick to allow them to guide us when our eternal destiny is at stake? In fact, nowhere in God's word are we told that our feelings/emotions are a sufficient guide to right and wrong. On the contrary we are told that only the scriptures can accomplish that task and we are to rely on the scriptures as our guide in serving God.

> *Romans 10:17 So then faith comes by hearing, and hearing by the word of God.*

> *Psalm 119:105 Your word is a lamp to my feet And a light to my path.*
