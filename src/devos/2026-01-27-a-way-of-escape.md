> **2 Peter 2:6-9** And turning the cities of Sodom and Gomorrah into ashes, condemned them to destruction, making them an example to those who afterward would live ungodly; and delivered righteous Lot, who was oppressed by the filthy conduct of the wicked (for that righteous man, dwelling among them, tormented his righteous soul from day to day by seeing and hearing their lawless deeds)-- then the Lord knows how to deliver the godly out of temptations and to reserve the unjust under punishment for the day of judgment,

In Peter's letter of reminders, he provides not only reminders of God's warnings against those who disobey. But Peter also provides us with reminders of encouragement -- both are necessary for all Christians. We need to understand that God will punish us -- but equally we need to be assured that He can and does bless the righteous.

Have you ever considered that in that great story of God's indignation against homosexuality and perversity -- His judgment and destruction of the centers of those sinful activities, there is a message of hope? A message of encouragement and promise and that message comes from God's dealings with Lot. Peter says that Lot lived in a sinful society and all the sin that surrounded him troubled his soul greatly. And because of his righteousness God offered to save Lot and his family from judgment. We know the story, how they fled to the mountains, but Lot's wife turned to look back and was turned into a pillar of salt. Peter says this was God's deliverance. And the story of Lot reminds you and I that just as God protected Lot from sinful influence he can protect you and I today by providing us a way of escape.

> **1 Corinthians 10:13** No temptation has overtaken you except such as is common to man; but God *is* faithful, who will not allow you to be tempted beyond what you are able, but with the temptation will also make the way of escape, that you may be able to bear *it*.
