> **2 Peter 3:14-16** Therefore, beloved, looking forward to these things, be diligent to be found by Him in peace, without spot and blameless; and consider that the longsuffering of our Lord is salvation---as also our beloved brother Paul, according to the wisdom given to him, has written to you, as also in all his epistles, speaking in them of these things, in which are some things hard to understand, which untaught and unstable people twist to their own destruction, as they do also the rest of the Scriptures.

How sad it is when man can't understand what the Bible says OR when he **doesn't want** to understand the truth of God's word. He then goes about the business of changing what is written to fit his opinion or desire.

Paul said it this way, "*For the time will come when they will not endure sound doctrine, but according to their desires, because they have itching ears, they will heap up for themselves teachers; and they will turn their ears away from the truth and be turned aside to fables.*"

God doesn't MAKE man hear and understand that's left up to man. And the arrangement of the Bible is such that it takes ***devotion*** and ***love -- for truth & God*** -- to understand. Without these two attitudes we will fall into the same pattern as those described by Peter & Paul. Looking for a way to change the parts we don't like or don't want to obey. How do we avoid these dangerous attitudes? Well, the answer is found in Bible study. The more we study the more God helps us to develop the proper attitudes that lead to the proper understanding of what is written. Without study we'll find ourselves looking for the wrong answer and we'll find it -- to our destruction.
