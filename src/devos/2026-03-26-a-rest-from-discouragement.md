> *Hebrews 4:9-10 There remains therefore a rest for the people of God. For he who has entered His rest has himself also ceased from his works as God did from His.*

One of the greatest battles God's children fight in this life is the battle against discouragement. We are surrounded by the sinfulness of the world and wonder if mankind will ever straighten up. Sometimes we are discouraged because we see the wicked and it seems like they never lose, never have problems and always seem to live prosperous and peaceful lives. Or maybe we are discouraged with our own weaknesses and the seeming impossibility to overcome problems, bad habits, or bad choices in our lives. Maybe we become discouraged because of some lengthy illness in ourselves or in those we love.

If you can remember your American History you remember that the early pioneers looked westward for a new home and new life. They saw in California, Oregon & Wyoming the promised land. During the 1800s people from the east poured westward as fast as the wagons would take them always moving toward their goal. Always looking toward, the "setting sun," because that was where they were longing to be. We need to keep our eyes on our goal, which is HEAVEN and we can see that place through the eyes of faith and what we see is.. a place of rest -- Not a cessation of any and all activity but a place of rest from the toils and trials of life here on earth where we are tempted on every side and have to work hard to overcome. The rest we will enjoy is a rest from SIN and all its consequences. Most certainly there will be a rest from discouragement.

> *Hebrews 4:11a Let us therefore be diligent to enter that rest,*
