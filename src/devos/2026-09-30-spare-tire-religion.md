> **James 1:21** Therefore lay aside all filthiness and overflow of wickedness, and receive with meekness the implanted word, which is able to save your souls. **(26)** If anyone among you thinks he is religious, and does not bridle his tongue but deceives his own heart, this one's religion is worthless.

A preacher once asked this question to the congregation he was speaking to and then asked, "Was it..." SPARE TIRE religion -- a religion that is only used in case of an emergency? WHEELBARROW religion -- a religion that was easily upset and one that has to constantly be pushed? Or BUSreligion -- religion that was ridden only when it was going your way? Or TALK RADIO religion -- were people only talk about being religious.

James explains to us that there are only two types of religion. A religion that pleases God and results in His blessings and man's salvation. Or, vain religion, a religion that has no value at all in one's relationship with God. The Greek word from which religion is translated literally means "the fear of God." For Christians this fear is recognized and blessed by God because of one's devotion to obeying God, worshipping God, and service to others.

> **Matthew 22:37-40** Jesus said to him, "'You shall love the LORD your God with all your heart, with all your soul, and with all your mind.' This is the first and great commandment. And the second is like it: 'You shall love your neighbor as yourself.' On these two commandments hang all the Law and the Prophets."
