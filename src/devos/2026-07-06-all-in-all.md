> **Luke 14:25-26** Now great multitudes went with Him. And He turned and said to them, **26** "If anyone comes to Me and does not hate his father and mother, wife and children, brothers and sisters, yes, and his own life also, he cannot be My disciple.

Occasionally we sing, "*Jesus is all the world to me, my live, my joy my all. He is my strength from day to day; without Him I would fall.*" The song truly expresses the attitude that Jesus expects from His disciples. Jesus demands that He is our "*all in all.*" With no exceptions. We come to Jesus with all the attachments in the world. Family, work, education, friends, children etc. etc. But as we grow spiritually the world and its attachments must decrease, and our dependency on Jesus must increase until we have forsaken all and Jesus become all the world to us.

> **Luke 14:33** "So likewise, whoever of you does not forsake all that he has cannot be My disciple.
