The Poet writes:

> "Trying to walk in the steps of the Savior,  
> Trying to follow our Savior and King;  
> Shaping our lives by His blessed example,  
> Happy, how happy the songs that we bring.

> **1 Peter 2:21** For to this you were called, because Christ also suffered for us, leaving us an example, that you should follow His steps:

The word example here could properly be translated OVERWRITING. And this is seen in the classroom where the teacher is teaching children to write. The teacher uses the first line of the paper to produce the "overwriting" or example. And the closer the child writes to the OVERWRITING the more accurate their letters will be! In much the same way Peter is telling all of mankind that being like God means we are staying close to the overwriting and the overwriting is Christ! Therefore, we should follow in His steps. Christians by virtue of having put off the old man of sin (Ephesians 4:22), and taking upon ourselves the new man (Ephesians 4:23,24), are to make every effort to live in such a way that God is glorified and that is accomplished when we walk in the steps of Jesus. Because walking in the steps of Jesus is a "life style" not an occasional action or thought.

> **Matthew 16:24-26** Then Jesus said to His disciples, "If anyone desires to come after Me, let him deny himself, and take up his cross, and follow Me. For whoever desires to save his life will lose it, but whoever loses his life for My sake will find it. For what profit is it to a man if he gains the whole world, and loses his own soul? Or what will a man give in exchange for his soul?
