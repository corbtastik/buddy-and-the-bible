> **Hebrews 6:17-19** Thus God, determining to show more abundantly to the heirs of promise the immutability of His counsel, confirmed it by an oath, that by two immutable things, in which it is impossible for God to lie, we might have strong consolation, who have fled for refuge to lay hold of the hope set before us. This hope we have as an anchor of the soul, both sure and steadfast, and which enters the Presence behind the veil,

The Christians, the writer speaks to here, were undergoing some storms of life, severe storms that threatened their faith. If they lose their faith -- the destruction will include their souls for an eternity. But as he says we have refuge, a place of safety and protection that we can anchor our faith and our eternal destiny to. And that anchor is the hope and assurance that comes from God's sure and precious promises. Just as God's promises to Abraham were true and came about. His promises to us for strength and guidance are just as true & sure to be fulfilled.

> **Hebrews 13:5-6** Let your conduct be without covetousness; be content with such things as you have. For He Himself has said, "I will never leave you nor forsake you." So we may boldly say: "The LORD is my helper; I will not fear. What can man do to me?"
