> **Psalm 5:4-5** For You *are* not a God who takes pleasure in wickedness, Nor shall evil dwell with You. The boastful shall not stand in Your sight; You hate all workers of iniquity.

"Love me? Love me fat, love my cat," I've heard some people say. I have also heard people say, "you have to accept me AS IS, God does." With the flood of phobias in our society it seems good that some folks are not obsessed to the point of physical and mental illness about outward appearances. But on the other hand, there is a real downside to this accept me as I am, attitude too. Especially, when it comes to our relationship with God. Many people think that when we come to God for salvation that God should and does accept us as they are. They contend God loves us unconditionally and will accept sinners as they are. Most certainly God loves all mankind even those who are His enemies (John 3:16; 1 John 4:10; Romans 5:10). But to say God accepts the sinner without a person's willingness to change his/her life falls short of some very important Bible principles. The very nature of God requires His rejection of anyone and everyone who chooses to live their lives "as they please." The very purpose of the gospel is to promote change in the heart of a sinner.

> **Acts 17:30-31** "Truly, these times of ignorance God overlooked, but now commands all men everywhere to repent, because He has appointed a day on which He will judge the world in righteousness by the Man whom He has ordained. He has given assurance of this to all by raising Him from the dead."
