> **Genesis 19:23-26** The sun had risen upon the earth when Lot entered Zoar. Then the LORD rained brimstone and fire on Sodom and Gomorrah, from the LORD out of the heavens. So He overthrew those cities, all the plain, all the inhabitants of the cities, and what grew on the ground. But his wife looked back behind him, and she became a pillar of salt.

> **Luke 17:31b-32** And likewise the one who is in the field, let him not turn back. Remember Lot's wife.

Notice Jesus words remember Lot's wife. Lot's wife had the opportunity to escape from destruction, she left with her husband. But looked back in violation to God's commands and became the object of His wrath along with all in Sodom and Gomorrah.

What is most impressive about the story of Lot's wife is that she so nearly escaped from death. She was going in the right direction. But then there was that moment. When she stopped and turned her head to look back and was turned into a pillar of salt. Why? Most obviously because, even though she was convinced to leave Sodom, she had not been converted to the danger to her life. Her feet were on the road to safety and life, but her heart was in the city.

Pleasing God demands all or nothing, all our heart or none of it, all our mind or none of it; all our soul or none of our soul. We cannot serve God and serve ourselves at the same time.
