> **Matthew 5:8** Blessed are the pure in heart, for they shall see God.

It is interesting how the Greeks used the word from which we get this word PURE. Originally the Greek word simply meant CLEAN and could, for instance be used to describe clothing that was dirty but had been laundered, washed clean. It came to be used to describe corn that had been winnowed or sifted and cleansed of all chaff. Removing all the useless material from the grain. In the same way it was used to describe an army which had been purged of all discontented, cowardly, unwilling and inefficient, ineffective soldiers becoming a force composed of only first class fighting men. It was also used to describe milk or wine that had not been diluted with water. Or of metal that was completely free of any alloys.

So we might define the word pure as something that is unmixed, unadulterated, or unalloyed. Something that has not been defiled with other matter changing the basic nature of the element. When applied to the heart this would mean a heart that is free from any and all thoughts, motives & intentions that defile man and cuts him off from fellowship with God. Bible purity is synonymous to holiness consider how the Hebrew writer says it.

> **Hebrews 12:14** Pursue peace with all people, and holiness, without which no one will see the Lord:

Holiness means literally being separated, separated from sin and separated from the world free from the contaminates of sinfulness -- you see it's the same thing as purity.

> **2 Corinthians 6:17** Therefore "Come out from among them and be separate, says the Lord. Do not touch what is unclean, And I will receive you."
