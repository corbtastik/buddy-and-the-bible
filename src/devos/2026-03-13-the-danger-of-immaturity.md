> *1 Corinthians 13:11 "When I was a child, I spoke as a child, I understood as a child, I thought as a child; but when I became a man, I put away childish things"*

Paul's statement brings to mind our responsibility as Christians to "grow" (2 Peter 3:18). The Hebrew writer likens our Christian life to that of an individual from youth to maturity (Hebrews 5:13-14). Indeed, we see in the New Testament our Spiritual life beginning at birth - when we are baptized into Christ. We are seen at first as children, who during the process of time and effort, become mature.

If we think about it enough, we are able to understand the parallel to physical life. We reflect on our own lives and remember in the past when "we acted like children". But as we grew and matured, we stopped doing those silly things and acted more our age. The great danger of spiritual immaturity is susceptibility. We become susceptible to any and every false doctrine that comes down the pike (Ephesians 4:14). We become listeners of men rather than of God. We are swayed by what pleases us rather than what is beneficial for "sound" spiritual health.

Growth then becomes a matter of desiring God's word as a babe longs for milk (1 Peter 2:2). God's word studied and applied will keep us going and keep us growing. It will create and maintain in our hearts a godlike attitude that will be reflected in our godly living.
