> **Matthew 18:6-9** "But whoever causes one of these little ones who believe in Me to sin, it would be better for him if a millstone were hung around his neck, and he were drowned in the depth of the sea. "Woe to the world because of offenses! For offenses must come, but woe to that man by whom the offense comes! If your hand or foot causes you to sin, cut it off and cast it from you. It is better for you to enter into life lame or maimed, rather than having two hands or two feet, to be cast into the everlasting fire. And if your eye causes you to sin, pluck it out and cast it from you. It is better for you to enter into life with one eye, rather than having two eyes, to be cast into hell fire."

In Matthew 5 Jesus tells us that we need to remove all things from our lives that might tempt us to lust after things we cannot lawfully participate in. Even if that separation is difficult and maybe painful, we must eliminate those temptations. In Matthew 18 within the context of causing believers to sin, Jesus tells us that we should remove all things that might be a stumbling block for others. Temptations that would lead them to either choosing not to follow the Lord or might discourage them to the point they leave The Faith or lose their faith altogether. What Jesus is teaching is that there are times in every Christian's life that we must make decisions that are difficult, inconvenient, personally undesirable and sometimes even painful -- to keep ourselves on the "straight and narrow" path to eternal life. Not only for ourselves but for those with whom we have direct contact and influence over. Don't kid yourselves: not only will we each give an account of our actions but also how our decisions affected and influenced those around us.
