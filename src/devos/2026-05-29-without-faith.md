> **Hebrews 11:6** "And without faith it is impossible to please Him, for he who comes to God must believe that He is and that He is a rewarder of those who seek Him"

Not only does faith allow us to please God, but it is *impossible* to please God without it. Because faith includes *believing* in God, *trusting* in God, and *obeying* God. These are all necessary in order to please God.

We must **believe in** God in order to please Him. Because refusing to believe in the existence of God, one is never moved to honor Him.

We must **trust** in God in order to please Him. The wise man said, "*Honor the Lord from your wealth and from the first of all your produce*". To honor God from the *first* of one's produce demonstrated that He would continue to provide even after the first of the produce was received.

We must **obey** God in order to please Him. Remember the example of the Israelites and how they regularly disobeyed God (1 Corinthians 10:5-11). Their disobedience was directly tied to their lack of faith.

Our purpose in life is to please God. And God mercifully shows us the means of doing just that. It begins in the mind and will and is seen in the way one lives.
