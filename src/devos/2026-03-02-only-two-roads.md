> **Matthew 7:13-14** "Enter by the narrow gate; for wide *is* the gate and broad *is* the way that leads to destruction, and there are many who go in by it. Because narrow *is* the gate and difficult *is* the way which leads to life, and there are few who find it.

Jesus warns us that the vast majority of people will spend forever and ever in the eternal fires of hell that will never be extinguished. Mankind cannot comprehend how horrible hell is going to be. Jesus also tells us there are only two roads to eternity and everyone is on one or the other. If we miss heaven, we will be punished in the eternal fires of hell forever. One of the saddest things about being condemned to an eternity in hell is the fact that it could have been avoided. People will be lost in spite of what the Lord has done. Hell is not what God does to us, hell is what one does to himself. A person who ends up in hell is his own worst enemy and only has himself to blame. Let us make going to Heaven our number one priority in this life.
