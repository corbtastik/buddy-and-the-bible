> **James 4:7-10** Therefore submit to God. Resist the devil and he will flee from you. Draw near to God and He will draw near to you. Cleanse *your* hands, *you* sinners; and purify *your* hearts, *you* double-minded. Lament and mourn and weep! Let your laughter be turned to mourning and *your* joy to gloom. Humble yourselves in the sight of the Lord, and He will lift you up.

Sometimes we as Christians are guilty of ASSUMING we are a safe distance from the devil. We make a stand, we overcome a temptation and then we feel smug that we have it all together and fail to take the necessary next steps to distance ourselves further from danger. Yes, we have to run away from the devil but we need to make sure we are running in the direction of God and not in a circle back to Satan.

Drawing near to God means growing to love Him based upon the fact that He loves you. And the most wonderful thing about His love is that I don't deserve it, but He loves me anyway.

Cleansing ourselves from sin begins with godly sorrow. Far too many boast about their sin rather than being sorry for offending God and sinning against the one that loves us. If you are not sorry for your sins the door of your heart is wide open for Satan to make a home there.

Humble yourselves before God begins by realizing that God is LORD OF ALL and we are willing to submit to God's directions and instructions rather than our desires and emotions. God becomes first, in our thinking and living.

You see the closer we get to God the further we are from the devil and the further we are from danger.
