> **2 Corinthians 6:9-10** Now I rejoice, not that you were made sorry, but that your sorrow led to repentance. For you were made sorry in a godly manner, that you might suffer loss from us in nothing. For godly sorrow produces repentance leading to salvation, not to be regretted; but the sorrow of the world produces death.

Sometimes, as unpleasant as it may be, to get well we must endure some painful treatments. Shots, root canals, surgery etc. If successful we enjoy better health afterward.

The sickness of sin requires some painful experiences as well before one can be healed from the choices we make. That's the point Paul makes, *godly sorrow* is in fact a painful experience that can really hurt, and if one doesn't experience godly sorrow, he might question whether or not his repentance is genuine.

But to get to the point where godly sorrow actually exists, requires some other painful experiences. A person must be convinced and convicted he/she has sinned. And being told that you are wrong is a painful thing -- no one likes to hear that. But then even after hearing from the Word that I'm wrong I have to be willing to admit I'm wrong. Admitting I'm wrong, also is painful because sin is an offense against God, our Heavenly Father, who loves us and wants only the best for us. When we face that fact, our heart is open for our need for God's mercy.

Godly sorrow is a painful step that leads to a painless eternity.
