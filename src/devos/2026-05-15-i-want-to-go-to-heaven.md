> **2 Corinthians 5:1** For we know that if our earthly house, this tent, is destroyed, we have a building from God, a house not made with hands, eternal in the heavens.

> **Revelation 14:13** Then I heard a voice from heaven saying to me, "Write: 'Blessed are the dead who die in the Lord from now on.'" "Yes," says the Spirit, "that they may rest from their labors, and their works follow them."

> **Revelation 21:4** "And God will wipe away every tear from their eyes; there shall be no more death, nor sorrow, nor crying. There shall be no more pain, for the former things have passed away."

I want to go to heaven, more than anything else. And the older I get the more I understand that nothing in life can compare to what heaven is going to be. Not one thing that I have or have experienced or have done compares even a small little bit to the glory and splendor that awaits me in heaven. We know very little about heaven, but I once heard a theologian describe it as "an unknown region with a well-know inhabitant," and there is not a better way to think of it than that. The hope of Heaven is that one great motivating force for living righteously, resisting temptation, enduring any and all hardships which are necessary for furthering the Gospel and for remaining joyful and optimistic in all circumstances even in the face of death. If we really understood what God offers we would avoid sin at all cost and we would be diligent to avoid carelessness and apathy. Let us look beyond the present to the future that God is wanting to share with us!
