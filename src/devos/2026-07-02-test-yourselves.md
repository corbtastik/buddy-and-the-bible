> **1 Corinthians 10:12** "Let him who thinks he stands take heed lest he fall".

> **2 Corinthians 13:5** "Test yourselves to see if you are in the faith; examine yourselves."

When we are so convinced that we are right that we are no longer open to truth seeking bible study, we are in "sur-nuf" trouble. Because it's easy to become blind to our own shortcomings or even worse justify the things we know are wrong in our lives. The test that truthfully reveals where we stand with the Lord, requires genuine humility and an eagerness to be like Jesus. There are no shortcuts or "half-ways" to accomplishing holiness. Being holy requires honesty, diligence, and an undying love for truth.

> **2 Thessalonians 2:9-12** The coming of the lawless one is according to the working of Satan, with all power, signs, and lying wonders, and with all unrighteous deception among those who perish, because they did not receive the love of the truth, that they might be saved. And for this reason God will send them strong delusion, that they should believe the lie, that they all may be condemned who did not believe the truth but had pleasure in unrighteousness.
