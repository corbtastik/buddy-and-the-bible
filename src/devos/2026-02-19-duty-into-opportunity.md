> **1 Chronicles 29:16-17** "O LORD our God, all this abundance that we have prepared to build You a house for Your holy name is from Your hand, and *is* all Your own. I know also, my God, that You test the heart and have pleasure in uprightness. As for me, in the uprightness of my heart I have willingly offered all these *things;* and now with joy I have seen Your people, who are present here to offer willingly to You.

David was dedicated from the early part of his reign to preparing to build the temple, which was what his life was all about. Before we will serve God willfully and cheerfully, we too must have dedicated our lives to Him with all our might. Nothing can be more important than Jesus, serving Him must fill our minds and lives. Total dedication is the only thing that pleases God because it produces a willing attitude in the heart of man. Willingness also comes from a loving heart. David loved God and his goal was to honor God in the building of the Temple. His actions became more than obligatory, he wasn't acting because he had to, or out of fear of consequences if he didn't. That's what dedication and love does, they change duty into opportunity.
