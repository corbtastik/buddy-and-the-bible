> **Colossians 2:13** And you, being dead in your trespasses and the uncircumcision of your flesh, He has made alive together with Him, having forgiven you all trespasses,

Can you imagine a legal system that will release a criminal repeat offender, 226 times? The system in Lincoln Nebraska released a Mr. Kevin Holder after he had been arrested 226 times. Records indicate his rap sheet was over 43 pages long. Remarkable isn't it?

Think about this and be honest, how many times has God forgiven you of your sins? Reckon your spiritual "rap sheet" would be longer than Mr. Holder's criminal rap sheet? What the numbers indicate is the enormity of God's grace and the vast amount of His love. He is willing to forgive if we are willing to repent. And He will forgive again when we stumble again but then repent from a contrite heart. He will forgive again, and again as many times as we are willing to admit our sin and do our best not to sin again. Shouldn't that motivate us to do better, struggle more intensely to overcome when we are tempted? Reminding ourselves of how much God has forgiven us helps us to do better in our service to Him.

> **Psalm 103:8** The LORD is merciful and gracious, Slow to anger, and abounding in mercy.
