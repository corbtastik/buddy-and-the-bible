> *Luke 8:25 But He said to them, "Where is your faith?" And they were afraid, and marveled, saying to one another, "Who can this be? For He commands even the winds and water, and they obey Him!"*

Jesus miraculously calmed a windstorm on the Sea of Galilee, and by doing so, to strengthen the faith of His disciples. Jesus had fallen asleep in the boat, and the fearful disciples thought Jesus was unconcerned as the boat filled with water. Fear seized their hearts and exposed the smallness of their faith. The Lord's question to them is as powerful as it is insightful. Jesus had much work to do. Did they think a windstorm would prevent Him from accomplishing it? Faith in Jesus assures us that even in the face of trouble, His will shall prevail. So, "where is your faith?" Doubt weakens and disables faith when coupled with fear. Faith's joy during trial and trouble is possible because we know the Lord will accomplish His will. Faith in Christ trusts His power to fulfill His will. The winds and water obeyed Jesus when He commanded them. Live by faith (not by sight) by obeying Jesus when He commands you. Bold faith obeys Jesus through the storm because He is Lord of heaven and earth.

> *James 1:2-4 My brethren, count it all joy when you fall into various trials, knowing that the testing of your faith produces patience. But let patience have its perfect work, that you may be perfect and complete, lacking nothing.*
