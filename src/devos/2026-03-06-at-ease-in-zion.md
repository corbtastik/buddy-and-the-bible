> *"Woe to them that are at ease in Zion, and trust in the mountain of Samaria, which are named chief of the nations, to whom the house of Israel came!"* (Amos 6:1, KJV)

A life of ease is the desire of many people. Having no concern over anything, being able to do what one wants to do when they want to do it. God's people in the day of Amos had developed an attitude of ease. Figuring they were God's chosen they had nothing to worry about. They went about their business and basically forgot about God. Amos gives the people a warning in chapter five regarding their idolatry and sloth. He concludes this chapter by prophesying about coming captivity.

This text should teach us a valuable lesson about our service to God. We should never be "at ease" with what we do or how we are living. One preacher I know says the life of a Christian is lived "on the incline" meaning it is one that must always be moving forward. Growth is commanded given to every Christian, and it simply is not easy. A lady once said to a well-known preacher, "I'd give half my life to be able to understand and quote the Bible like you do." To which the preacher replied, "Sister, you're trying to get off easy." Let us not become satisfied and haughty but ever move forward in our service.

> *2 Peter 3:18 but grow in the grace and knowledge of our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ.*
