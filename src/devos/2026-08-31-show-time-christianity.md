> **Galatians 2:11-13** Now when Peter had come to Antioch, I withstood him to his face, because he was to be blamed; for before certain men came from James, he would eat with the Gentiles; but when they came, he withdrew and separated himself, fearing those who were of the circumcision. And the rest of the Jews also played the hypocrite with him, so that even Barnabas was carried away with their hypocrisy.

When Ashton was born, she was so different than Ally and Alex that we called her the "drama queen" even when she was very little. She had learned very early that she got a lot of attention, and everyone thought she was so cute when she was dramatic. So, every emotion, request and complaint was exaggerated and of course we all laughed, ooh-ed & ahh-ed and by doing so encouraged her even more until we have created this precocious exuberate, and sometimes very entertaining young lady. We are a society that is very caught up in entertainment; it seems to be the center of our lives at times. Actors and actresses are looked upon as national heroes because of their contributions to our "fun" and entertainment. But you know acting is not high on God's list of "acceptable activities." What I mean by that acting like one is a Christian.

You wonder sometimes if Sundays aren't just show-times for many folks. They show up, go through all the motions of worship, shake hands and after they leave the building, it's like they leave their Christianity there. Go their way and live their lives as they wish, doing as they please, until its show-time again.

> **Revelation 22:14-15** Blessed are those who do His commandments, that they may have the right to the tree of life, and may enter through the gates into the city. But outside are dogs and sorcerers and sexually immoral and murderers and idolaters, and whoever loves and practices a lie.
