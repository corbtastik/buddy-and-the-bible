> **Esther 6:5-6** The king's servants said to him, "Haman is there, standing in the court." And the king said, "Let him come in." So Haman came in, and the king asked him, "What shall be done for the man whom the king delights to honor?" Now Haman thought in his heart, "Whom would the king delight to honor more than me?"

As the plot thickens in the story of Esther, Haman, the man who convinced the king to have all the Jews in his kingdom destroyed, enters the palace, just as the king wants someone to honor Mordecai, who had some years before saved his life. Our text is the king asking for advice from his trusted advisor about how Mordecai might be honored. But notice Haman's attitude. He thinks he's the only one deserving of honor and the king is talking about him. "*Whom would the king delight to honor more than me?*" It is sad that Haman's attitude is shared by far too many within the family of God. Individuals who have promoted themselves above others for some reason or the other. The truth is if we are going to build one another up, we have to place ourselves below others rather than above. Humility is honored by God and He has His ways of humbling those who think too much of themselves.

> **1 Peter 5:5** Likewise you younger people, submit yourselves to you] elders. Yes, all of you be submissive to one another, and be clothed with humility, for "God resists the proud, But gives grace to the humble."
