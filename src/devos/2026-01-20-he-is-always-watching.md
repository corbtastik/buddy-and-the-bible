> **Psalm 139:1-4** O LORD, You have searched me and known *me*. You know my sitting down and my rising up; you understand my thought afar off. You comprehend my path and my lying down, and are acquainted with all my ways. For *there is* not a word on my tongue, *But* behold, O LORD, You know it altogether.

We have this great weakness of mind we think when no one is watching us we can do things and get by with them. If my doctor's not watching I can eat as much sugar as I want and he won't ever know -- but the reality is when he goes through the procedure of blood testing, he can tell just how much sugar I've had. When I was 12, I thought I could go out in the woods and smoke cigarettes, and Mom & dad would never know. But even when I was standing in the yard Mom (in the house) could smell the smoke on me and she knew I had been smoking.

We do this with so many other areas of life. When not around other Christians we talk, dress, and act as we please regardless of what God instructs or commands -- because we feel if our brethren, our spouse, or family can see us we are free from guilt. On the other hand, it's easy when we are around those folks to control our tongue, control our thoughts and actions -- because we are being watched.

David reminds us that *God is always with us*. I mean each and every moment He is there watching us, the dark doesn't hide us, the closet doesn't hide us -- being alone doesn't hide us from God HE IS ALWAYS WATCHING. Think about that for a moment, because right now He knows your thoughts.
