> **Philippians 1:27** Only let your conduct be worthy of the gospel of Christ, so that whether I come and see you or am absent, I may hear of your affairs, that you stand fast in one spirit, with one mind striving together for the faith of the gospel,

Christians, those who profess the gospel of Christ. Should live their lives in agreement with that which we believe and confess. One of the purposes of all religions is to have some governing influence upon the living of those who subscribe to it. The difference in Christianity is its influence stems from the righteous character of our creator. Peter wrote we are to be holy as God is Holy. Paul instructs us to be conformed to the image of Christ. Having a Godlike nature means being in the world while not being of the world. And there are some very practical reasons for us to do these things. God is glorified in our Holy living when we willingly choose Him rather than self. Holy living honors the new nature Christians have are recreated into. Holy living also sustains our hope for eternal life. So, it becomes those who believe gospel truths, submit to gospel laws, and depend upon gospel promises, to live a life comparable to that which the gospel declares. I mean what good is professing belief in something that doesn't have an effect on your lives? One fellow says that kind of faith is a matter of convenience instead of conviction! Christianity is a practical religion one that forms our thinking and molds our living.

> **Romans 12:1-2** I beseech you therefore, brethren, by the mercies of God, that you present your bodies a living sacrifice, holy, acceptable to God, which is your reasonable service. And do not be conformed to this world, but be transformed by the renewing of your mind, that you may prove what is that good and acceptable and perfect will of God.
