> **Psalm 18:2-3** The LORD is my rock and my fortress and my deliverer; My God, my strength, in whom I will trust; my shield and the horn of my salvation, my stronghold. I will call upon the LORD, who is worthy to be praised; so shall I be saved from my enemies.

> **Psalms 20:7** Some trust in chariots, and some in horses; But we will remember the name of the LORD our God.

In In the last scene of The Movie "Saving Private Ryan." When the squad was defending a single bridge until relief would arrive. The American Lieutenant after being severely wounded in the last throws of life lays on the bridge with the German tank coming towards him takes out his 45-caliber US Army Pistol and begins firing at the tank! And when he fires the last round in perfect time with an air strike, the tanks explodes and the Lt. looks at the pistol with a look that seemed to say it took five bullets! He was confident that he could make a difference with just a handgun. David with just 5 stones faced Goliath, confidence that with God's help he would be victorious.

A Christian's confidence doesn't come from one's own strength, ability or craft, but from God, His promises and His ability to provide anything and everything we need to face the challenges of life and be victorious. Like David who wasn't confident in his ability but in God's ability to strengthen, teach and direct him in battle. God has provided us everything we need, and it is up to us to stand and persevere with faith.
