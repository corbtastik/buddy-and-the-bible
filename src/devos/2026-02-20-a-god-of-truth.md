> **Deuteronomy 32:3-4** For I proclaim the name of the LORD: ascribe greatness to our God. *He is* the Rock, His work *is* perfect; for all His ways *are* justice, a God of truth and without injustice; righteous and upright *is* He.

> **John 14:6** Jesus said to him, "I am the way, the truth, and the life. No one comes to the Father except through Me.

God is a God of truth, it is a part of His divine person. Jesus is described as the source and the embodiment of truth. As His children we are assured of His promises because He is faithful and cannot lie.

To become a child of God one must be "born of the truth" through the process of hearing the gospel and obeying the truth therein. It is that incorruptible seed that "makes us like our father". Just as the genetic qualities are passed along through the physical reproduction process, so are divine qualities passed along through the spiritual birth process. So if we are born of God we then become "like" God, truthful and lovers of truth. Man is saved by obedient faith, saving faith is based upon the truth of God's word. Faith that is based upon anything else but truth it is of no value to our salvation. Actions that are the results of hearing a perverted gospel are not the responses that please God and save.

Truth, we must hear it, obey it and speak it. Then and only then can we say we are God's children.
