> **Matthew 7:13-14** "Enter by the narrow gate; for wide *is* the gate and broad *is* the way that leads to destruction, and there are many who go in by it. Because narrow *is* the gate and difficult *is* the way which leads to life, and there are few who find it.

When just a boy I rode my bicycle everywhere, to school and from school. The ride home from school was the easiest because I could coast the entire three miles. Downhill all the way. Going to school was another story altogether.

We are tempted to coast in our Christianity in several ways and it seems after certain significant events in our Christianity. Many think that after they have obeyed the gospel and have been baptized that the work is done and efforts to seek God and obey Him cease -- I've read Robert Turner's I've Ben Baptized which sadly enough depicts how many feel.

Others think that after a particular spiritual struggle, like problems in the family or in the church or at work or with the neighbors etc. They have weathered that particular storm and now they have earned the right to kick back and take it easy for awhile.

Some think that after a period of time, through Bible study and experiences in life that they have mastered Christianity and feel they can overcome trials and temptations pretty successfully and because of that feel no compunction to continue to work in study, attendance, prayer or other areas of service to the Lord.

Then as we said there are some who by virtue of serving the Lord for a lifetime feel that they deserve the right to retire from their work serving God and the brethren.

The point is Christianity is not easy nor does the challenge ever cease as long as we live life under the sun. Our rest comes after we've finished traveling the "narrow way."
