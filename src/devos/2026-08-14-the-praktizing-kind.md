> **Matthew 18:2-5** Then Jesus called a little child to Him, set him in the midst of them, and said, "Assuredly, I say to you, unless you are converted and become as little children, you will by no means enter the kingdom of heaven. Therefore whoever humbles himself as this little child is the greatest in the kingdom of heaven. Whoever receives one little child like this in My name receives Me.

Jesus sets down some principles here that His disciples at one time didn't like and certainly the world isn't willing to accept today, and that includes many who are claiming to be Christians. Samuel Butler writes "People in general are equally horrified at hearing the Christian religion doubted, and at seeing it practiced."

In the Movie "Sergeant York" Alvin York has been told he had to register for the conscription, but he answers the Preachr Pile, "the good book says it er wrong to be a'killin other folks and war is a'killin and I can't be a goin to war!" Too which Preacher Pile says "Alvin most folks have got the meetin house kind of religion, but you have got the PRAKTIZING KIND"

Many so-called Christians fight like mad to defend the name, organization, and work of the church, and stand against false teachers. But absolutely refuse to follow these teachings of Jesus in matter of personal character and attitudes. Jesus states without question that for one to be a member of His kingdom a person must become like a Child. He must turn from pride and turn to humility. Because greatness will only come when we are willing to become like children. We have to have the "praktizing" kind of Christianity.
