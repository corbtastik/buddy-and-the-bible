> "Is it for me dear Savior, Thy glory and Thy rest, for me, so weak and sinful! How can I be so blest? Is it for me, Thy welcome, Thy gracious 'Enter in,' for me Thy 'come, ye blessed,' for me so full of sin."

The writer of this well known hymn speaks to the heart of those whose desire is to be with the Lord, but who's faith from time to time wavers.

Our faith, if not firmly fixed, can easily be damaged by problems of everyday life, even family life; it can be hurt by dis-obedient Christians, by rumors and half-truths; our faith can be overthrown by agents of Satan!

Hear what Paul says about what can happen to us and our faith, in his first letter to young Timothy, Paul tells us that our faith can be, "shipwrecked, departed from, denied, cast off, strayed from." All of these things can happen to us and to our faith. With this understanding, we may wonder, "Is it for me? Can I go to heaven? Maybe it is hopeless?"

We need to stop right here! The Bible is not a book of hopelessness! If we read these verses and are discouraged, then maybe we have misunderstood the point of these verses. Maybe we need to read some other verses. The Bible is a book of encouragement. The Savior's answer comes back, "Yes! Heaven is for you!"

> **Hebrews 6:10-12** For God *is* not unjust to forget your work and labor of love which you have shown toward His name, *in that* you have ministered to the saints, and do minister. And we desire that each one of you show the same diligence to the full assurance of hope until the end, that you do not become sluggish, but imitate those who through faith and patience inherit the promises.

We have a hope; that hope is the anchor that keeps the soul and keeps our faith! Our hope is heaven.
