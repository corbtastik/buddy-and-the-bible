> **John 13:14-16** If I then, your Lord and Teacher, have washed your feet, you also ought to wash one another's feet. For I have given you an example, that you should do as I have done to you. Most assuredly, I say to you, a servant is not greater than his master; nor is he who is sent greater than he who sent him.

> **Matthew 20:26-27** "Yet it shall not be so among you; but whoever desires to become great among you, let him be your servant. And whoever desires to be first among you, let him be your slave---

Being a servant means "looking past yourself; not demanding one's rights; going first and going alone; serving those who don't understand , even those who don't care." (copied)

Being a servant is not what most folks like to think of themselves. Sadly, many Christians shutter at the thought of lowering themselves into the position of a servant. Even when they are helping someone who needs help they still want to be elevated into a position of importance. "I've done a good deed, look at me" kind of attitude. That's not the kind of servant Jesus was nor is it the kind of servant He expects His disciples to be. Humility is an essential attitude of being a Christian. To be seen, to be recognized, to be praised because of serving might make you feel good about yourself, but it doesn't please Jesus.

> **James 4:10** Humble yourselves in the sight of the Lord, and He will lift you up.
