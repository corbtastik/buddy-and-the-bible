> **Hebrews 2:1-3** Therefore we must give the more earnest heed to the things we have heard, lest we drift away. For if the word spoken through angels proved steadfast, and every transgression and disobedience received a just reward, how shall we escape if we neglect so great a salvation, which at the first began to be spoken by the Lord, and was confirmed to us by those who heard Him,

The writer here uses a mariner's term, (drift) that speaks of the movement of a vessel on the water, driven by winds and currents gradually moving away from its prescribed course of travel. The most important man on the ship when traveling becomes the navigator. Because he's the fella that going to get you to that small speck of land in the middle of the ocean, or to that safe harbor across the ocean from where you began. What happens if the navigator fails to read the GPS correctly & pay close attention to the wind, or the currents, if he doesn't take these readings regularly it's not long until the ship is off course and maybe even straying into dangerous waters.

So it is with Christians we are traveling a course set by God that leads to heaven and eternal salvation. And in the course of living, the danger of drifting off course is real and threatening to every one of us!

> **Hebrews 3:12** Beware, brethren, lest there be in any of you an evil heart of unbelief in departing from the living God;

The danger is that we can drift so far away from God that we come to completely disregard God's word and when that happens, we have nothing whatsoever to move our hearts and minds to repentance and when we have no place for repentance in our heart there is no chance of salvation. (c.f. Heb. 6:4-6)
