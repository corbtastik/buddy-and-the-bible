Just a little prayer for the family

Dear father in heaven, all praise glory and honor be to you! We bow before your throne of grace and mercy giving thanks to you for being so loving and compassionate and caring and kind, for giving us your word that has the power to change our lives and give us wisdom to live our lives in a way that is truly rewarding and meaningful and beautiful. Dear Lord we live in a dark world, we are surrounded by many devices of the enemy, he is working so hard to destroy your children, we pray earnestly that you protect us, that you enlighten our minds to see the true path, that you give us strength and courage to overcome the temptations and constant barrage that he presses against us. Show us the way Lord, show us your love and mercy, forgive us our sins and bring us to the mountain on high. Bless our hands as we work for you, give us opportunities to serve you and strengthen us to be more like Jesus who has such compassion and love, who looked to the weak and damaged and broken people and extended himself even to death to help them. Help us to Love like Him to be humble like like Him and to be brave like Him-

Amen
