> *2 Corinthians 11:3 but I fear, lest somehow, as the serpent deceived eve by his craftiness, so your minds may be corrupted from the simplicity that is in Christ.*

It doesn't take a lot of observation of scripture to understand that the devil wants control of our mind. He wants or minds to be blinded from the truth. He wants our minds to be blinded through the various temptations of this world. And he wants our minds to be corrupted by lies and deceit. There is a great and mighty battle going on in the universe and it is a battle between good and evil it is a battle for the mind of man! Satan on one side and God on the other. Every human being eventually makes that decision, and the results determine who & what we are. Whether you will live your life guided by man's wisdom or God's wisdom. Either one will affect the way you live and will determine where you spend eternity. We are what we think and if we are not careful, our soul will be lost for all eternity.

To win the battle and assure our salvation we must arm ourselves with the power of God's word. Only with God's help can we make the choice to submit to "*the mind of Christ*" rather than the corruption of the devil.

> *Romans 1:16 For I am not ashamed of the gospel of Christ, for it is the power of God to salvation for everyone who believes, for the Jew first and also for the Greek.*
