> **Matthew 5:14-16** "You are the light of the world. A city that is set on a hill cannot be hidden. Nor do they light a lamp and put it under a basket, but on a lampstand, and it gives light to all *who are* in the house. Let your light so shine before men, that they may see your good works and glorify your Father in heaven.

> **Ephesians 5:13-14** But all things that are exposed are made manifest by the light, for whatever makes manifest is light. Therefore He says: "Awake, you who sleep, Arise from the dead, And Christ will give you light."

My son Brad played football when in High School and if you remember pep rallies always preceded the Friday night games. A group of mothers of football players got together and planned a "Pep Rally Moms of FB Players" routine for one of the pep rallies, and Joy was invited in. During the planning stages some of the women suggested putting PMS across the front of their shirt and across the backside of their shorts. Joy stood up and said she wasn't doing anything that accentuated body parts nor act in bad taste. Because she had the courage to speak up several other of the mothers had the courage to do the same and the uniform design was changed appropriately. This is only an illustration of how "letting the light of Christ" shine in our lives influences others to turn from darkness to His light in their lives. So "awake" and be determined to "let your light shine" today and every day. And may God be glorified in our doing.
