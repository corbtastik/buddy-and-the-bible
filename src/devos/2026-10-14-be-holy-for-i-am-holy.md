> **1 Peter 1:13-17** Therefore gird up the loins of your mind, be sober, and rest your hope fully upon the grace that is to be brought to you at the revelation of Jesus Christ; as obedient children, not conforming yourselves to the former lusts, as in your ignorance; but as He who called you is holy, you also be holy in all your conduct, because it is written, "Be holy, for I am holy." And if you call on the Father, who without partiality judges according to each one's work, conduct yourselves throughout the time of your stay here in fear;

Christians, those who profess the gospel of Christ. Should have their manner of living as becomes that gospel or live in agreement to that which we believe and confess. One of the purposes of all religions is to have some governing influence upon the living of those who subscribe to it. The difference in Christianity is its influence stems from the righteous character of our creator. As Peter wrote we are to be holy as God is Holy. As Paul instructs us we are to be conformed to the image of Christ. Having a Godlike nature means being in the world while not being of the world. And there are some very practical reasons for us to do these things. God is glorified in our Holy living -- the whole purpose of man being created was to glorify God by our willingness to choose Him rather than self. Holy living honors the new nature Christians have been created in Christ. And Holy living sustains our hope for eternal life.

> **Jude 3** Beloved, while I was very diligent to write to you concerning our common salvation, I found it necessary to write to you exhorting you to contend earnestly for the faith which was once for all delivered to the saints.

One writer said if religion is worth anything it's worth everything -- the gospel is worth striving for. Sometimes we measure the worth of abstract things such as freedom by man's willingness to fight and die for it -- most military historians agree that the fiercest fighters of the past and present are those who are motivated by common ideals. Christianity is worth our contending for because it's standards are noble, it's purpose righteous, it's goals glorious.
