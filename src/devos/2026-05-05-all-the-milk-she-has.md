> **Matthew 22:37** Jesus said to him, "'You shall love the LORD your God with all your heart, with all your soul, and with all your mind.' 38 "This is [the] first and great commandment.

President Dwight Eisenhower once told a story about a Kansas Farmer who was trying to sell a cow. The buyer asked the Farmer about the cow's pedigree, butter fast production, and monthly production of milk. The farmer said, "I don't know what a pedigree is, and I don't have an idea about butterfat production, but she's a good cow, and she'll give you all the milk she has." This is the kind of attitude each of us must have in our service to God. We must be willing to give Him our ALL. One writer said, "My potential is God's gift to me. What I do with my potential is my gift to Him." If we give our very best all the time we can make our lives a bright reflection of the light of Christ, that may in some way lead a lost soul to His salvation.

> "You are the light of the world. Let your light so shine before men, that they may see your good works and glorify your Father in heaven."
