> **Galatians 2:20** "I have been crucified with Christ; it is no longer I who live, but Christ lives in me; and the life which I now live in the flesh I live by faith in the Son of God, who loved me and gave Himself for me. **(5:24)** And those who are Christ's have crucified the flesh with its passions and desires.

I read about a young boy who received in the mail a record that he had ordered. When he opened the package, he found that the company had failed to perforate a hole in the record. So, the boy decided to make his own hole. It's not hard to guess what happened. The hole that the boy made was so off-center that the record swaggered and wobbled all over the turntable. And the sound? It was horrible!

This is exactly what happens when we ignore God's design for our lives and make up our own rules. This is what the Bible calls sin. Sin operates life with self, not God, as the center. When we place that which is imperfect (self) at the center and allow our lives to revolve around ourselves - we are off balance. The result? Discord and frustration, heartache and insecurity for ourselves and those around us, as we wobble through our self-centered lives.
