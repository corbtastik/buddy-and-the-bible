> **Mark 4:40** But He said to them, "Why are you so fearful? How is it that you have no faith?"

Some things by nature are opposites -- light and darkness, hot and cold, good and bad. Fear and faith fall into that same category. When caught on the Sea of Galilee when a powerful storm suddenly moved in, the Twelve were terrified and begged Jesus to wake up and do something. After He had calmed the sea with just three words "Peace, be still" he asked the Apostles why they were afraid, had they not witnessed many of His miracles, had He not fed thousands with only small portions of fish and bread? The problem they had was a lack of faith in Jesus. Jesus reverses things, He makes darkness light, He changes bad persons into good persons. He changes that which is old into something that is new, the dead to be alive, the lost to be found. Jesus can, if we are willing, free us from fear by and through faith Him. Faith in Jesus does away with fear and keeps us moving on the "straight and narrow" way to salvation.

> **Hebrews 2:14-15** Inasmuch then as the children have partaken of flesh and blood, He Himself likewise shared in the same, that through death He might destroy him who had the power of death, that is, the devil, and release those who through fear of death were all their lifetime subject to bondage.
