> **1 Corinthians 13:11** "When I was a child, I spoke as a child, I understood as a child, I thought as a child; but when I became a man, I put away childish things"

> **Ephesians 4:31-32** Let all bitterness, wrath, anger, clamor, and evil speaking be put away from you, with all malice. And be kind to one another, tenderhearted, forgiving one another, even as God in Christ forgave you.

We have all seen children get into some fuss; who with furrowed brows and loud screams stand nose to nose accusing the other of something while the other is trying to out-scream and out-accuse the other. They stomp their feet, call one another names, and make terrible threats. Spiritual immaturity leads to the same kind of action's in the church. Fussing and fighting is a sign of spiritual immaturity. God's children are those who "*endeavor to keep the unity of the faith in the bond of peace*" by putting others before themselves and allowing God's word to be the solution to their problems rather than their feelings.

> **Galatians 5:22-23a** But the fruit of the Spirit is love, joy, peace, longsuffering, kindness, goodness, faithfulness, gentleness, self-control.
