> **1 Peter 1:3-5** Blessed be the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, who according to His abundant mercy has begotten us again to a living hope through the resurrection of Jesus Christ from the dead, to an inheritance incorruptible and undefiled and that does not fade away, reserved in heaven for you, who are kept by the power of God through faith for salvation ready to be revealed in the last time.

Notice how Peter describes the "living hope" we have in Christ Jesus. He doesn't tell us what heaven is, he tells us what heaven is not. Heaven isn't corruptible, it isn't defiled, and it will not fade away. And heaven it the object of our hope.

But as we follow Peter's declaration of our living hope we find in verse thirteen that there is a necessary response to this promised hope.

> **1 Peter 1:13-16** Therefore gird up the loins of your mind, be sober, and rest your hope fully upon the grace that is to be brought to you at the revelation of Jesus Christ; as obedient children, not conforming yourselves to the former lusts, as in your ignorance; but as He who called you is holy, you also be holy in all your conduct, because it is written, "Be holy, for I am holy.

Since we are "begotten again to a living hope" we are to be about the business of:

- Preparing our minds by removing any and all sinful thoughts and doubts that might hinder our service to God.
- Be sober in thought and purpose, seriously devoted to doing all we are instructed to do.
- We are to be obedient children, which simply means being committed to following Jesus' teachings and example.
- And we are to follow the example of our Heavenly Father we are to be holy, separated from self, and the sinful world. We are to be devoted to being righteous as God is righteous.

> **Hebrews 6:11-12** And we desire that each one of you show the same diligence to the full assurance of hope until the end, that you do not become sluggish, but imitate those who through faith and patience inherit the promises.
