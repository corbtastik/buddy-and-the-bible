> **Matthew 21:22** "And all things, whatever you ask in prayer, believing, you will receive,"

What powerful words, what a wonderful promise what a great responsibility!

Prayer has been from the beginning and still remains one of the most important parts of man's relationship to God. It is both a blessing and a responsibility. We are gifted with the *right* to approach the Creator of all things the one true and living God and talk to Him as a friend, as a child would speak to his Father. Such a great blessing.

But also prayer is a *responsibility* because our relationship with God cannot and will not survive the cruel attacks and temptations of Satan if man does not take the time to pray, "*without ceasing.*" And our relationship with others will never be what God expects it to be if we do not spend time in prayer on behalf of our neighbor.

The Bible tells us that God hears ONLY the prayers of those who meet the criteria and conditions He has placed upon listening to us. John tells us that if we "*ask according to His will*" God will hear us *(1 John 5:14)*. He also says that God hears those who "*keep His commandments and do the things that are pleasing to Him*" *(1 John 3:22)*. John also records that God does not hear the prayers of sinners but only those who "*do his will,*" *(John 9:31)*.

Notice the condition Jesus, Himself, puts upon prayer -- BELIEF. Meaning we must believe that God hears and that He answers our prayers and His answers will be the best thing for us!
