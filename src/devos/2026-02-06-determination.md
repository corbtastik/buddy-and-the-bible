> *Genesis 50:15-20 When Joseph's brothers saw that their father was dead, they said, "Perhaps Joseph will hate us, and may actually repay us for all the evil which we did to him.., Then his brothers also went and fell down before his face, and they said, "Behold, we are your servants." Joseph said to them, "Do not be afraid, for am I in the place of God?..,But as for you, you meant evil against me; but God meant it for good, in order to bring it about as it is this day, to save many people alive.*

**DETERMINATION** -- *is defined as "a firmness of purpose"* Firm intent or a decision that has been made.

Determination is the strength of mind to keep following a planned course or direction in spite of the pitfalls or setbacks one may suffer. Joseph is a great example of determination, after being sold by his brothers into slavery. Unjustly imprisoned by Potipher because of the lies of his two timing wife. Forgotten by the servant of Pharaoh whose future he foretold. Joseph remained faithful to the Lord. Every Christian stumbles and falls, sins even though we may hate sin. But determination keeps us from giving up and giving in completely to the devil -- we must be determined to "remain faithful unto death." We must be determined to worship as God commands. We must be determined to avoid the very appearance of evil. Determined to choose friends that help us grow spiritually. Determined to remain faithful even when we make the same mistake over and over again. Because we know God will forgive and Christ's blood will cleanse us.
