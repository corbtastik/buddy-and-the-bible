> **Matthew 13:33** Another parable He spoke to them: "The kingdom of heaven is like leaven, which a woman took and hid in three measures of meal till it was all leavened."

Of all the parables that Jesus taught this is one of the shortest. A lesson on the power of God's rule on the hearts of man. Leaven is used throughout the scripture to illustrate influences. Jesus and Paul warned of bad influences.

> **Matthew 16:6** Then Jesus said to them, "Take heed and beware of the leaven of the Pharisees and the Sadducees."

> **1 Corinthians 5:6** Your glorying is not good. Do you not know that a little leaven leavens the whole lump?

Jesus in this parable is teaching of the power of the Gospel to change the heart of man. The illustration is this, when yeast is added to dough it causes tiny bubbles to form in the dough so that when the dough is baked the bread is light and tender. When the gospel is planted in the good and honest heart it begins to change the thinking and consequently the actions of the individual. One ceases to be guided by selfish wants and desires and becomes a follower and imitator of Jesus. So when the gospel is planted in the heart of man, when accepted, changes a person from an unbeliever into a believer; from one unconcerned about life-style choices to one determined to be righteous; from a rebellious person to one willing to submit themselves to the "rule of God."
