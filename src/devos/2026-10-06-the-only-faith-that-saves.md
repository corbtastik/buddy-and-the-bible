---
title: "The Only Faith That Saves"
date: 2026-10-06
time: 8:10 AM
scripture: "Romans 10:17"
summary: "Faith built on the words of men -- even good, honest, Christian folks -- is not the faith that saves."
---
> **Romans 10:17** So then faith comes by hearing, and hearing by the word of God.

> **Acts 17:11-12a** These were more fair-minded than those in Thessalonica, in that they received the word with all readiness, and searched the Scriptures daily to find out whether these things were so. Therefore many of them believed,

For all the good qualities that man has we also show ourselves to be very weak in many areas. We want to believe that there is good in everyone to a fault. We tend to judge people by their looks and overlook some who are have good character and accept those who have very poor characters. Also, we tend to accept what we hear from people, on TV, on the radio or internet, without taking the time to see if indeed what has been said is true.

Children are taught to mind their parents and accept their instructions and when they become old enough to begin reasoning for themselves some of that trust & reliance stays with them especially in matters pertaining to religion. How many times have you heard someone say, "I'm a Baptist, Methodist, Pentecostal...(etc.) because my parents were?" What has occurred is those people accepted the words of their parents about what is right and wrong religiously and their faith was based upon their words. And it happens to people in the church of Christ as well. It is a common problem for Christians to accept things as true without really putting them to the test. A young man said to me once in a discussion about a certain question, "I believe what so & so said, because I trust him to tell the truth." And indeed, many do tell the truth but even those we trust can sometimes be less than truthful. And even if they are not if we fail to investigate then our faith is based upon their words and not God's word. The only faith that saves is faith that is the product of God's words not the words of man, even if we believe them to be good, honest, Christian folks.
