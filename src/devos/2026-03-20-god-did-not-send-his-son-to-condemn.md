> *John 3:16-17 "For God so loved the world that He gave His only begotten Son, that whoever believes in Him should not perish but have everlasting life. For God did not send His Son into the world to condemn the world, but that the world through Him might be saved.*

One of the greatest qualities of God is Love, it's part of His nature, His thinking and His actions. It is a part of Himself that He can't deny! And that quality of love directs God always to think of others! To be compassionately aware of their needs and willing to consider those needs even though the ones in need aren't worthy of consideration. His only concern always is for the good of others. The Devil, on the other hand, would have all believe that God's law is unloving, restrictive, restraining anyone and everyone from that what we want to have. You can't do this, you can't do that, you can't do anything as long as you're a Christian! -- This is what many people think God is like and His law is like -- He wants to keep us from having fun. Why does God restrict us from these activities, because He doesn't want us to enjoy life or have a good time? No because true happiness is found in following His instructions.

> *John 10:10 "The thief does not come except to steal, and to kill, and to destroy. I have come that they may have life, and that they may have it more abundantly.*
