> **Hebrews 11:11** "By faith Sarah herself also received strength to conceive seed, and she bore a child when she was past the age, because she judged Him faithful who had promised."

> **1 John 5:4** For whatever is born of God overcomes the world. And this is the victory that has overcome the world--our faith.

A dear friend of mine recently said, "I just pray for strength to do what is right.." In our time of confusion, it really should be everyone's prayer to be strong enough to do what is right even when doing right is difficult. So, we seek strength from God. And the fact of the matter is God gives us that strength through faith.

That word "*strength*" in the NKJV and KJV comes from the same word that the word "*power*" is translated from in Romans 1:16 "*I not ashamed of the gospel of Christ for it is the power of God to salvation..*" Strength, power, or the ability to accomplish -- all of these are the result of "*judging Him faithful who promised*" -- having faith. "*The assurance of things hoped for and the conviction of things not seen.*"

If we want to have strength to overcome the doubt and fear of this world, we must *have* faith and we must *strengthen* our faith (through study, prayer & worship) because it is only through that faith that we can be victorious.
