> **Luke 19:5-6** And when Jesus came to the place, He looked up and saw him, and said to him, "Zacchaeus, make haste and come down, for today I must stay at your house." So he made haste and came down, and received Him joyfully.

Jesus was traveling through Jericho and the crowds are immense to the point that this short man was unable to see the Lord so he climbs a tree to get a better view and Jesus purposefully passes under that tree, stops and looks up and calls Zacchaeus by name. I can't help but believe Zacchaeus didn't look around to make sure the Lord was speaking to him. Jesus knew Zacchaeus and He knows each one of us. He knows our situation and he knows where we are in relationship to Him, if we are His He knows that, if we are not He knows that too and because He knows us He can provide just what we need to make our life right and make us a part of His family, its up to us to accept His provisions and instructions.

> **2 Timothy 2:19** Nevertheless the solid foundation of God stands, having this seal: "The Lord knows those who are His," and "Let everyone who names the name of Christ depart from iniquity."
