> **Nehemiah 8:2-3** So Ezra the priest brought the Law before the assembly of men and women and all who could hear with understanding on the first day of the seventh month. Then he read from it in the open square that was in front of the Water Gate from morning until midday, before the men and women and those who could understand; and the ears of all the people were attentive to the Book of the Law.

Several years back Archaeologist, found and dug an ancient city up in Palestine. What was interesting about the find was the discovery of 62 lbs. of ancient jewelry, worth thousands of dollars. What was interesting about the discovery is it was buried only eighteen inches under the floor of a cottage that had been continually lived in for over 500 years. For centuries people had been living only inches from a great treasure, oblivious to its existence.

Isn't it sad that the same thing occurs today, spiritually? The treasure of God's word in many cases is only inches away from almost everyone and people are oblivious to the treasure they have access to. A treasure of more value than any amount of gold or silver that exists. A treasure that offers eternal life to those who hear, believe and obey its message. The question we should ask ourselves is just how much value do I place on God's word? Am I interested in the great treasure God has provided?
