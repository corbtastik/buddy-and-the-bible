> **Matthew 3:7-9** *But when he saw many of the Pharisees and Sadducees coming to his baptism, he said to them, "Brood of vipers! Who warned you to flee from the wrath to come? "Therefore bear fruits worthy of repentance, and do not think to say to yourselves, 'We have Abraham as our father.' For I say to you that God is able to raise up children to Abraham from these stones.*

Isn't it interesting how the religious leaders of John and Jesus' day thought God's law didn't apply to them? For some reason they felt exempt from the commands and principles they taught. The Pharisees regarded themselves special and because of that the gospel of repentance didn't apply to them. Like the self-righteous Pharisee who prayed with himself, *"...I thank you I am not like other men."* It was always others that needed to change, others that God condemned for sinfulness. Therefore, when John came preaching repentance, they just didn't think it applied to them!

Sadly, that same kind of attitude is alive and well among the people who have named the name of Jesus and claim to be God's children. Oh yes, they believe that baptism is necessary for salvation. No, we can't have musical instruments in worship. Yes, we must meet each and every Lord's Day and worship! But when principles are taught that apply to personal lives and attitudes it's always "that applies to someone else, not me!" So be careful the next time you condemn someone for gossiping, or hatred, or being a busybody etc., and then turn right around and do those same things yourself.

> **Romans 2:1-3** *"Therefore you are inexcusable, O man, whoever you are who judge, for in whatever you judge another you condemn yourself; for you who judge practice the same things. But we know that the judgment of God is according to truth against those who practice such things. And do you think this, O man, you who judge those practicing such things, and doing the same, that you will escape the judgment of God?"*
