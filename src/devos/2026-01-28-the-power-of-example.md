> **Matthew 18:6-9** "But whoever causes one of these little ones who believe in Me to sin, it would be better for him if a millstone were hung around his neck, and he were drowned in the depth of the sea. "Woe to the world because of offenses! For offenses must come, but woe to that man by whom the offense comes!

When Paul commands Christians to "examine themselves" he was giving one of the most difficult of commands for individuals to obey. Because man has a tough time looking at himself honestly. We can recognize other people's faults but we have a tendency to go easy on ourselves.

Now Jesus is warning us that we need to be very, very careful with our words, actions and attitudes because if we cause someone to sin, we are in deep trouble with the Lord. Being chunked into the sea and receiving a "woe" from God is serious business. So we are to be very careful in our relationships with our brethren ("ones who believe in Me").

How is it that we might cause our brothers of sisters to sin? Think about the power of example, are you living a life that encourages or discourages others? Is your devotion to assembling and worshipping an example of complete dedication or half-heartedness? When in worship are doing your best to "teach and admonish" our brethren or do you simply want to get done and get out? In worship is God your focus or are your entertained by others actions or lack of actions?

Yes we can be or become the reason someone else sins and Jesus strongly condemns that lack of concern and love for His disciples.
