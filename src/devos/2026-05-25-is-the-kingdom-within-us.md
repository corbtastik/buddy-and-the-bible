> **Luke 11:2ff** Our Father in heaven, Hallowed be Your name. Your kingdom come. Your will be done On earth as it is in heaven. Give us day by day our daily bread. And forgive us our sins, For we also forgive everyone who is indebted to us. And do not lead us into temptation, but deliver us from the evil one."

> **Ephesians 5:17** Therefore do not be unwise but understand what the will of the Lord is.

Homer Hailey in his book on "Prayer and the Providence of God," makes a statement that caught my attention. Looking at the model prayer commonly called the "Lord's Prayer" where Jesus taught "your kingdom come your will be done on earth as it is in heaven." Hailey reminds us that we cannot prayer this verbatim because the Kingdom of Christ was established on Pentecost after Jesus' death, burial & resurrection. But we are to pray for the kingdom, and the best way we can learn how to pray for the kingdom is to understand what the kingdom is. Not only does understanding its nature help us to better pray for it but it also helps us recognize whether or not "the kingdom is within us."

> **Luke 17:20-21** Now when He was asked by the Pharisees when the kingdom of God would come, He answered them and said, "The kingdom of God does not come with observation; nor will they say, 'See here!' or 'See there!' For indeed, the kingdom of God is within you."

We should ask ourselves, is God ruling in our heart or does He share the throne with SELF?
