> **Psalm 139:5-7** You have hedged me behind and before, and laid Your hand upon me. *Such* knowledge *is* too wonderful for me; it is high, I cannot *attain* it. Where can I go from Your Spirit? Or where can I flee from Your presence?

David recognized the protective presence of God, "hedged about." From top to bottom and side to side God covers us with His protective presence.

When I was younger schools weren't so accommodative to students in giving them a ride to school. Some folks actually had to walk to school. And we walked even when the weather was cold and rainy. Mom didn't hesitate to send my brother and I out to school if it was raining because we had some really cool Red-Rider rain suits. There was a hat, a long coat, pants and rain boots to match. I mean we could walk all the way to school in a pretty good rainstorm and stay dry. Because we were protected from top to bottom, we were wrapped in rainproof clothes. Now after we passed the 3rd & 4th grade it wasn't cool to wear Red-Rider stuff, it was cooler to get wet.

But God is like that raincoat, if we allow Him through faith and obedience. He will "*hedge us about*" with His presence. God promises to UPHOLD us in times of temptation, not allowing us to be tempted beyond what we can bear. God promises to uphold us in times of persecution, by assuring us of His reward to those who are faithful unto death. God promises to uphold us even when everyone else abandons Him and His way with God's help I CAN REMAIN faithful to Him and His word.
