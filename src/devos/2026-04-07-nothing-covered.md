> *Luke 12:1-3 In the meantime, when an innumerable multitude of people had gathered together, so that they trampled one another, He began to say to His disciples first of all, "Beware of the leaven of the Pharisees, which is hypocrisy. For there is nothing covered that will not be revealed, nor hidden that will not be known. Therefore whatever you have spoken in the dark will be heard in the light, and what you have spoken in the ear in inner rooms will be proclaimed on the housetops.*

Every sin we attempt to hide is uncovered and open before the eyes of God, even when we succeed in concealing them from others. The light of His truth reveals our innermost thoughts and the sins that result from them. When David hid his sins, he experienced increased grief and pain, but he found forgiveness and the joy he had lost, when he acknowledged his sin to God. Let us follow his example instead of Achan, who could not conceal his sin nor escape its punishment.

Today and everyday, remember and think about the truth that God knows all about us, and His truth will save us when we open our hearts to His word and follow His will. Paul reminds us to live in the light of truth,

> *1 Timothy 5:24-25 "Some men's sins are clearly evident, preceding them to judgment, but those of some men follow later. Likewise, the good works of some are clearly evident, and those that are otherwise cannot be hidden"*
