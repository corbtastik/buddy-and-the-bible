---
title: "Confirmed, Not Standby"
date: 2026-06-25
time: 6:48 AM
scripture: "Hebrews 3:6"
summary: "The difference between a confirmed ticket and flying standby is the confidence factor."
---
What is the difference between a passenger who is flying out of town with a confirmed ticket and a passenger who is flying out of town but flying on standby? I don't fly but I can suppose that one would be calmly awaiting his/her flight while the other might be nervous, hanging around the ticket counter wondering if someone will or has canceled their seat. The difference is the confidence factor.

The Hebrew writer encourages Christians to remain faithful and in three places encourages us to remain confident in the salvation Jesus has provided.

> **Hebrews 3:6** but Christ as a Son over His own house, whose house we are if we hold fast the confidence and the rejoicing of the hope firm to the end. **14** For we have become partakers of Christ if we hold the beginning of our confidence steadfast to the end, while it is said:

> **10:35-36** Therefore do not cast away your confidence, which has great reward. For you have need of endurance, so that after you have done the will of God, you may receive the promise:

Confidence is directly related to our assurance. The assurance we have in God and in His promises and in His ability and willingness to keep those promises. In the world there are those whose assurance is based upon false hopes and false promises. But God's children have assurance based upon God's word, His promises and His unchanging nature. And with that assurance we can have confidence in our salvation and live our lives NOT worried about whether or not I may be saved but assured that if I believe in God and Jesus and I am doing my best to obey His commands -- I AM SAVED.
