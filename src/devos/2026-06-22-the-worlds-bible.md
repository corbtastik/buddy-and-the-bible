> **1 Thessalonians 1:6-10** You also became imitators of us and of the Lord, having received the word in much tribulation with the joy of the Holy Spirit, so that you became an example to all the believers in Macedonia and in Achaia. For the word of the Lord has sounded forth from you, not only in Macedonia and Achaia, but also in every place your faith toward God has gone forth, so that we have no need to say anything. For they themselves report about us what kind of a reception we had with you, and how you turned to God from idols to serve a living and true God, and to wait for His Son from heaven, whom He raised from the dead, that is Jesus, who delivers us from the wrath to come.

Father's Day, a great day to remember good fathers and show appreciation to them for the love, devotion and hard work, raising and providing for the family. But when I remember my father, I am reminded of his example. The importance of the Bible cannot be overemphasized. But before I was even able to read, I saw the bible lived out in the life of my father and mother. All my young life dad showed each of his children what the term father meant and because of his example later on, we gained a clearer picture of the nature of our heavenly Father. There is a song in our Book entitled "*The Worlds Bible*" and the second verse of that song reads. "*We are the only Bible the careless world will read, we are the sinner's gospel, we are the scoffers' creed, we are the Lord's las message given in deed and in word.*" And the words of the song are true, people will look at our lives and our attitudes long before they will read the Bible and indeed it may be our life that leads to seek to know more about God.
