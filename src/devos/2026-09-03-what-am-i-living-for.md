> **Ecclesiastes 2:18** "I hated all my labor in which I had toiled under the sun, because I must leave it to the man who will come after me. And who knows whether he will be a wise man or a fool? Yet he will rule over all my labor in which I toiled.."

There was a R&B song popular when I was young entitled *What Am I Living For*. It was a love song that asked the question, *what am I living for if not for you?* Meaning that the poet's life was centered on and dedicated to the one he loved.

Is our life wrapped up on our career, or our family? Do we live for our hobbies, recognition or position? There is certainly nothing wrong with these kinds of pursuits. You work hard you succeed and become successful, but what happens after you die? Not only that, but after death we must all stand before God and give an accounting of our living. At that point, our earthly bank account will not benefit us one whit! What are you living for, for the temporary rewards of wealth, position, or pleasure? Or are you living and longing for the eternal gifts reserved for those who live for Jesus?

> **Luke 12:18-20** "So he said, 'I will do this: I will pull down my barns and build greater, and there I will store all my crops and my goods.
