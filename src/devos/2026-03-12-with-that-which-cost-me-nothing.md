> *Romans 12:1 I beseech you therefore, brethren, by the mercies of God, that you present your bodies a living sacrifice, holy, acceptable to God, which is your reasonable service.*

> *Matthew 16:24 Then Jesus said to His disciples, "If anyone desires to come after Me, let him deny himself, and take up his cross, and follow Me.*

When David had sinned against the Lord in numbering the people and sought to appease God by sacrifice for his sin. He set out to purchase the threshing floor that belonged to Araunah. Araunah wanting to please his king offered the threshing floor and all else there to the king for free. But David replied *"No, but I will surely buy it from you for a price; nor will I offer burnt offerings to the Lord my God with that which cost me nothing."*

Sacrifice means just that, a giving up of something that is cherished or of value. God has demanded since creation that man "sacrifice" for Him. Christians are to present ourselves to the Lord as a "living sacrifice" and the cost of such a sacrifice is the denial of self. The implication of denying self is that we put everything else below Jesus and make Him first and foremost in our lives. Sacrifice is hard and God intended for it to be, so that the genuineness of our faith might be made known, to ourselves and others. There is nothing more important in time than for each Christian to live his/her life so that others can see our faith, our trust and our hope and understand those are the most important things in our lives.
