> *Psalm 39:4-5 "Lord, make me to know my end, and what is the measure of my days, that I may know how frail I am. Indeed, You have made my days as handbreadths, and my age is as nothing before You; certainly every man at his best state is but vapor.*

Time is not endlessly repeating itself. Every New Year's Day should remind us that the days add up, and after one day passes it's gone, never to return. Time is linear, not cyclical. The days do not repeat themselves; They are finite, and we lose them one by one. And as each day passes it brings us closer to an end. The Bible gives great and practical insight into this subject. So as one writer advises, "Instead of *"killing time"* we should be making wise use of every hour. Making the most of our limited time by focusing on those things that are most important: Beginning with our relationship with God! Because our relationship with God has eternal consequences:

> *Ephesians 3:15-16 Therefore be careful how you walk, not as unwise men, but as wise, making the most of your time, because the days are evil.*
