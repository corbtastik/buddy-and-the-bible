---
> **Isaiah 59:1-2** Behold, the LORD's hand is not shortened, that it cannot save; Nor His ear heavy, That it cannot hear. But your iniquities have separated you from your God; And your sins have hidden His face from you, So that He will not hear.

The prophet Isaiah was condemning the Jews for their wickedness in their departure from God's law. But as with all God's warnings and condemnations. There is God's plea for man to repent. God is sure to punish the sins of man, but always and forever eager to forgive, if the sinner will simply stop their wicked ways and turn back to following Him.

God's plea to man to repent affirms one major truth and that is sin is not God's fault. Too many people tend to blame God for their mishaps and mistakes. But God is not in the business of turning people to sin. God's interest in man is "*that all should come to repentance*". Sin is exclusively man's choice and because of that. When we all stand before God in judgment man will reap the harvest he has sown.

> **Galatians 6:7** Do not be deceived, God is not mocked; for whatever a man sows, that he will also reap.
