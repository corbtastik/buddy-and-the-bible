> **John 8:14** "You are of your father the devil, and the desires of your father you want to do. He was a murderer from the beginning, and does not stand in the truth, because there is no truth in him. When he speaks a lie, he speaks from his own resources, for he is a liar and the father of it.

Satan is the great deceiver, the father of lies. His tactic and his power over man is deception. He lies to deceive man into thinking that sin is right and that those who sin will get away with it! The question we must ask then is how do we escape deception? And the answer is so obvious that sometimes we overlook it and that is truth. Truth is the only effective means of fighting against deception and truth will always win over lies.

> **John 8:31-32** Then Jesus said to those Jews who believed Him, "If you abide in My word, you are My disciples indeed. And you shall know the truth, and the truth shall make you free."

Notice Jesus states that we can be free from sin and free from the deceptions of Satan if we are willing to: (1) **Know** the truth and then (2) **Abide** in that truth. We must be willing to accept the truth and then we must be willing to apply truth to our thinking and to our living. Then and only then will Satan not be able to deceive us!
