> **Hebrews 10:23-25** Let us hold fast the confession of our hope without wavering, for He who promised is faithful. And let us consider one another in order to stir up love and good works, not forsaking the assembling of ourselves together, as is the manner of some, but exhorting one another, and so much the more as you see the Day approaching.

While living in San Angelo, my mother after knee replacement surgery contracted a staph infection on her spine. My brothers and I took turns sitting with mother and there was one of the doctors that always seemed very interested in mother's well-being, he would always ask her "Are you getting what you need, Mrs. Payne?" I always felt that doctor was genuinely concerned about my mother.

The question is one that we should ask ourselves regarding our spiritual well-being. "Are we getting what we need?" To help us grow and strengthen our faith? Are we getting what we need to guide our thoughts, words and actions as we live each day of our lives? Are we getting what we need to get to heaven is the question we really should be asking ourselves.

And what we need spiritually is knowledge, encouragement and motivation to obey God's commands and the only way to acquire these needed things is by studying God's word, the fellowship of Christian brethren, and worship.

> **2 Timothy 2:15** Be diligent (study KJV) to present yourself approved to God, a worker who does not need to be ashamed, rightly dividing the word of truth.
