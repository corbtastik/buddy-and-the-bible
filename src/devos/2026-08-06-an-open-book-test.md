> **Proverbs 17:3** The refining pot is for silver and the furnace for gold, But the LORD tests the hearts.

> **2 Timothy 2:19** Nevertheless the solid foundation of God stands, having this seal: "The Lord knows those who are His," and, "Let everyone who names the name of Christ depart from iniquity."

Interestingly enough, God is a teacher that believes in tests and has given them to mankind since the beginning of time right up to today. And will continue to pass out the exams until the end of time.

Test fundamentally defined means "an action taken to discover truth or reality of a certain thing or situation." The test God gives and has given man is a test of man's faith. God reveals to man, instructions and commands. Man, who has the ability to choose an answer to God's test either believes and obeys God's instruction. Or he chooses to disregard and disobey His commands. One answer is correct the other incorrect. Both responses have consequences, obedience results in God's blessing. Disobedience results in God's wrath and punishment. God gives the tests and knows the answer man chooses, so it is put upon us to "choose wisely." BTW it's an open book test.
