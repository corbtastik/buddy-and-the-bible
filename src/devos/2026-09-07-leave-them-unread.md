> **1 Timothy 4:13** Till I come, give attention to reading, to exhortation, to doctrine.

Robert M. Hutchins said: "To destroy the western tradition of independent thought it is not necessary to burn the books. All we have to do is to leave them unread for a couple of generations"

To destroy proper spiritual thinking from the mind of Christians just leave the Bible unread for a couple of generations. I.E. I heard a young gospel preacher from a small east Texas town relate a story about an Elder from a church of Christ in that town, who actually went to the Baptist church went forward to make a statement apologizing for not being more tolerant towards other faiths. The reason that the ecumenical movement has the momentum it has in many churches of Christ, is because people are not reading the Bible for themselves. If you don't think the danger is real, you are not reading enough. God's word warns us of those who have accepted Christ, yet because of all kinds of different motives fall away and draw others with them. Ignorance of God's word creates spiritual weakness and at the same time gives strength to the devil's influence in our lives.

> **2 Peter 2:1-2** But there were also false prophets among the people, even as there will be false teachers among you, who will secretly bring in destructive heresies, even denying the Lord who bought them, and bring on themselves swift destruction. And many will follow their destructive ways, because of whom the way of truth will be blasphemed.
