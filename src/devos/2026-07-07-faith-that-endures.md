---
> **James 1:3** My brethren, count it all joy when you fall into various trials, knowing that the testing of your faith produces patience.

> **Peter 1:6-7** In this you greatly rejoice, though now for a little while, if need be, you have been grieved by various trials, that the genuineness of your faith, being much more precious than gold that perishes, though it is tested by fire, may be found to praise, honor, and glory at the revelation of Jesus Christ

Everyone understands the value of experience, learning by doing. Learning how to overcome and correct mistakes and then keep going. I believe that is the "patience" James speaks of, endurance and perseverance gained from experience.

Too many folks claim to have faith. They are faithful as long as being faithful is easy and painless. But when it becomes difficult to have that faith they lose it and sometimes, they lose it fast. Trials also help us learn to trust God and not ourselves. Trust is the attitude we find when things are beyond our control or out of our reach. When bad things happen, we turn to God for help. Genuine faith produces genuine trust. And genuine trust contributes to the perseverance we must have to overcome. When we learn to "let go and let God.." trusting that whatever happens God provides us a way of escape so that we can overcome. But it is up to us to use the "trial" to learn to trust God and accept His will.
