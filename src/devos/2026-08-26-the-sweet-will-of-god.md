We sing a hymn occasionally entitled "Sweet will of God" the first & third verse go like this;

> My stubborn will at last hath yielded; I would be Thine & Thine alone; and this the prayer my lips are bringing "Lord let in me Thine will be done."
>
> "Thy precious will, O conqu'ring Savior; Doth now embrace and compass me: All discords hushed, my peace a river, my soul a prisoned bird set free.

What's sad about those who refuse to give up their preconceived ideas, those who hold on to the family religion, or who want to wait until they can have all the fun they can have; or who simply don't want to be strapped down & kept from following their own path, is they miss out on the good and wonderful blessings of following Jesus. Jesus said, "*I have come that they may have life, and that they may have it more abundantly..*" But that abundant life is reserved and extended only to those who are willing to submit their will to the will of God.

> **Romans 12:1-2** I beseech you therefore, brethren, by the mercies of God, that you present your bodies a living sacrifice, holy, acceptable to God, which is your reasonable service. And do not be conformed to this world, but be transformed by the renewing of your mind, that you may prove what is that good and acceptable and perfect will of God.
