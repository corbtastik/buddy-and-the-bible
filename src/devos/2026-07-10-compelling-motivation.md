> **2 Corinthians 5:14-15** "For the love of God compels us, because we judge thus; that if One died for all, then all died; and He died for all, that those who live should live no longer for themselves, but for Him who died for them and rose again."

Paul says that it is God's love that compels "motivates us, pushes us forward, stirs up our will to act." God's love motivates us to overcome our fear; God's love motivates us to stop living for self, and His love inspires us to then begin living for Jesus. Each of us needs that "compelling motivation" every day and that is why prayer, Bible study and together worship is so vital to our spiritual life! We need to be reminded on a regular basis of God's love, so that our determination is renewed regularly --we don't just take our automobiles to the gas station once after we buy them, fill them up with gas and oil and expect that single serving to keep the car running for as long as we own it. We understand that it takes regular fill-ups & regular maintenance to keep our cars running.

The same is true spiritually it takes regular "fill ups" of God's word, prayer and together worship. So pray & study each day and attend every service of the Lord's body as if your eternal life depends upon it.

> **John 3:15-16** "And as Moses lifted up the serpent in the wilderness, even so must the Son of Man be lifted up, that whoever believes in Him should not perish but have eternal life. For God so loved the world that He gave His only begotten Son, that whoever believes in Him should not perish but have everlasting life.
