> *LORD, make me to know my end, And what is the measure of my days, That I may know how frail I am. Indeed, You have made my days as handbreadths, And my age is as nothing before You; Certainly every man at his best state is but vapor. Surely every man walks about like a shadow; Surely they busy themselves in vain; He heaps up riches, And does not know who will gather them. "And now, Lord, what do I wait for? My hope is in You.* **(Psalms 39:4-7)**

These scriptures were my father's favorite verses from the Psalms. He would quote them in every prayer he uttered until his memory failed him completely, but his memory of these scriptures was in fact the last to leave.

I thought of these verses and my father this morning as I read this thought. "*To the optimist, all doors have handles and hinges; to the pessimist, all doors have locks and latches.*" The pessimist might read Psalm 39 and think of only the shortness of life; the brevity of living and how quickly our time here on earth expires. The optimist on the other hand sees in these verses the importance of using our time wisely. Putting the life, we are blessed with to good use for our Lord. Working diligently to accomplish the good works ordained for us to accomplish. Seeking and recognizing opportunities to serve and be a servant. We have been blessed with another day, use it wisely and glorify God in the doing.
