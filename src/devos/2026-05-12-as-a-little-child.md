> **Luke 18:15ff** Then they also brought infants to Him that He might touch them; but when the disciples saw it, they rebuked them. But Jesus called them to Him and said, "Let the little children come to Me, and do not forbid them; for of such is the kingdom of God. Assuredly, I say to you, whoever does not receive the kingdom of God as a little child will by no means enter it."

Located between the Parable of the self-righteous Pharisee and the account of the money loving Young Ruler. The Holy Spirit guides Luke to record the account of people bringing little children to Jesus, "*that He might touch them..*" What is interesting when you compare the parable before and the account following you see two men who were NOT like a child. One was full of himself thinking because he kept parts of the law, he was a righteous, God accepted person. The other wasn't unlike the Pharisee because he too had a checklist religion. "*These (commands) I have kept from my youth.*" Both of those men failed to grasp what God really wants from everyone. A child is so connected to its parent that when you take a baby away from its mother, its cries to return to her arms. A baby even in a crowd can recognize its parents voice and again wants to be close to them. That's the kind of connection God wants from us. A loving desire to be with Him, and a recognition and acceptance of what He says.
