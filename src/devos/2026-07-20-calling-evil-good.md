> **Isaiah 5:20** Woe to those who call evil good, and good evil; Who substitute darkness for light and light for darkness; Who substitute bitter for sweet, and sweet for bitter!

> **Ezekiel 44:23** Moreover, they shall teach My people the difference between the holy and the profane and cause them to discern between the unclean and the clean.

One writer suggested that there are some important distinctions we should pay attention to: Men are not women, women are not men, pets are not kids, boyfriend & girlfriends are not spouses, the internet is not the local church, feelings are not facts, creatures are not the Creator, sin is not righteous.

I am afraid that far too many in our good land have lost a love for truth and would rather believe and accept what is not true. Be warned, God will allow us to believe whatever we want to believe, even to our eternal destruction. So, believe what you might, but be warned it is very possible to cross a point of no return. You may think you will always have time and opportunity to get right with the Lord but that is simply not true. The time is now and the opportunity exist only as long as one's heart is receptive to God's word.

> **John 12:39** He has blinded their eyes and hardened their hearts, lest they should see with their eyes, lest they should understand with their hearts and turn, so that I should heal them."
