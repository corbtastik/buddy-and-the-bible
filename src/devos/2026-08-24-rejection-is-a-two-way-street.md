> **Proverbs 1:24-26** Because I have called and you refused, I have stretched out my hand and no one regarded, because you disdained all my counsel, and would have none of my rebuke, I also will laugh at your calamity; I will mock when your terror comes,

Have you ever had an accident or experienced some heartache or tragedy and those who witnessed your accident or heard your troubles just laughed at your misery? It is a terrible thing that people can be so cold and unfeeling at the ill fortune of others.

But imagine (this may be easier for children) imagine you were a child and maybe fell down and really hurt yourself. It really hurts and there is no one there to help except maybe your dad. And you call for him and he looks at you and turns back to what he was doing. You cry and call louder but there is no reaction in fact he turns after a while and laughs at your pain and suffering. That is a picture of a hopeless situation, no help from anyone.

Here is God's warning to us. He has revealed His directions, His loving commands all which are for our good. Yet we choose to live our lives as we please. When some crisis occurs in our life and we need God's help and think He owes us His attention and assistance. God says that 'rejection' can be a two way street. We reject His instructions and commands and He will reject our pleas for help when we are suffering.

> **Proverbs 1:27-31** When your terror comes like a storm, and your destruction comes like a whirlwind, when distress and anguish come upon you. Then they will call on me, but I will not answer; they will seek me diligently, but they will not find me. Because they hated knowledge and did not choose the fear of the LORD, they would have none of my counsel and despised my every rebuke. Therefore they shall eat the fruit of their own way, and be filled to the full with their own fancies.
