> **Matthew 20:25-28** But Jesus called them to Himself and said, "You know that the rulers of the Gentiles lord it over them, and those who are great exercise authority over them. Yet it shall not be so among you; but whoever desires to become great among you, let him be your servant. And whoever desires to be first among you, let him be your slave --- just as the Son of Man did not come to be served, but to serve, and to give His life a ransom for many."

As usual the apostles were arguing about who's going to have the highest rank in the earthly kingdom, they thought Jesus had come to establish. So, Jesus uses the request of James and John's mother to teach an important lesson on discipleship. First, he brings out the fact that His kingdom is not going to be like the kingdoms of men -- an earthly kingdom. In Jesus' kingdom the winners are not the leaders but the followers. Those who are humble enough *to consider others more important than themselves*, who love their neighbor in the same way and with the same devotion that they love themselves. You see in Jesus' kingdom the greatest are those who are behind & beside

- Behind and pushing the wheelchair.
- Behind the lawn mower mowing the yard of the needy.
- Behind the vacuum, cleaning the home of the elderly.
- Behind the steering wheel driving the ill to the doctor.
- Behind holding the door so someone else can enter first.
- Beside the troubled, offering comfort and concern.
- Beside the lost, teaching them the gospel message.

Jesus said the greatest in the kingdom of heaven are those who are *willing to put others first and that begins by putting Jesus first in our lives*. And we do that by obeying His commands -- not the commands of men, not the commands of some religious organization -- but the commands of Jesus.
