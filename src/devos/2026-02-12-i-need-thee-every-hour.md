> **Psalm 121:1-2** I Will lift up my eyes to the mountains; From whence shall my help come? My help comes from the LORD, Who made heaven and earth.

You may be guilty of this as I am often. Instead of taking the time to teach someone how to do something, we just do it ourselves. I believe most of us have a "I can do it myself, I don't need any help" attitude about many things in our life.

Sadly, we allow that attitude to carry over into our relationship with Jesus and the truth is it is impossible for us to "do it ourselves" when it comes to pleasing God. Jesus said we are to choose the "difficult path" to eternal life (Matthew 7:13) and there is more than one reason He allows the road to be difficult, one is so that we will learn to be dependent on God for help. We have to rid ourselves of the "I can do it myself, I don't need any help" mindset and develop that "I need Thee every hour" mindset. God alone has the power to defeat Satan, and it is only God's power in us that overcomes the temptations the devil sets before us.

> "I need thee every hour stay thou nearby; temptations lose their pow'r when Thou are nigh.."
