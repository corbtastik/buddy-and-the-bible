---
title: "The Evidence of Things Not Seen"
date: 2026-11-06
time: 5:58 AM
scripture: "Hebrews 11:1"
summary: "Faith does not operate without evidence -- it rests on the recorded eyewitness testimony of the men to whom Jesus showed Himself."
---
> **Hebrews 11:1** Now faith is the substance of things hoped for, the evidence of things not seen.

Here is a good definition of a Christian's faith and notice what the writer tells us. He says that "faith is the evidence of things not seen." Now this doesn't mean that faith operates without evidence instead, evidence is the basis of our faith. Scriptures teach us that creation is evidence in the existence of God

> **Romans 1:20** For since the creation of the world His invisible attributes are clearly seen, being understood by the things that are made, even His eternal power and Godhead, so that they are without excuse.

What Paul is saying that just because some ignore or reject the evidence doesn't mean they are excused from believing in God. They'll not be able to stand before God on judgment and say, "there just wasn't enough evidence for me to believe you were real." Because the evidence is there, right before our eyes. What is interesting to me is that the Apostles had to learn the importance of secondhand evidence themselves before they were sent into the world to testify of the resurrection.

> **Luke 24:8-11** And they remembered His words. Then they returned from the tomb and told all these things to the eleven and to all the rest. It was Mary Magdalene, Joanna, Mary the mother of James, and the other women with them, who told these things to the apostles. And their words seemed to them like idle tales, and they did not believe them.

Why did God choose these women to be the first to witness the resurrection of the Lord. Because God wanted them to go and tell the Apostles about the resurrection. How did the Apostles react -- like most men -- these women are silly and don't know what they are talking about. It took Jesus appearing to them to convince them of the truth. But now these men were prepared to go into the world and give evidence to His resurrection and answer the skeptics based on their own experience.

The Bible calls upon us to believe based on the recorded evidence we have. The recorded eyewitness testimony of those men to whom Jesus presented Himself. And it is the Apostles word we have in written form that comprises the body of evidence upon which our faith is founded.

> **John 20:30-31** And truly Jesus did many other signs in the presence of His disciples, which are not written in this book; but these are written that you may believe that Jesus is the Christ, the Son of God, and that believing you may have life in His name.
