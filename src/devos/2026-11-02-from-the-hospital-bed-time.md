**From the Hospital Bed -- time**

When you are constrained in the hospital bed hooked up to all the IV's and tubes that are feeding you and giving you all the medicine the doctors order. You have a great deal of time especially during the night. The nurse says she'll be back in 30 so you watch the clock and when you watch the clock the time crawls by slowly. You have opportunity to think, reflect and pray. And one of the things I thought about was the passing of time.

> **Ecclesiastes 3:1** To everything there is a season, A time for every purpose under heaven:

> **Ephesians 5:15-16** See then that you walk circumspectly, not as fools but as wise, redeeming the time, because the days are evil.

One of the questions I asked myself was "Am I spending the time God has allotted me wisely?" Have you ever been asked that question? It is an interesting thought because we usually use the word spend to refer to money and how we use it, we either spend money or we save it. When we spend it, we are putting the money we have received into use. We are taking what we have and exchanging it for something else. For instance, when we receive our paychecks most of us deposit them in the bank. Then when we need something, we exchange that money for the product or service we need. When we are born a certain amount of time is deposited to our account, we are given a certain amount of time and as the days go by, we are using that time for some purpose its leaving our account like our money leaves our bank account, we are spending the time God gives. Now how are you spending your time? Each moment that passes has been taken out of your assets and placed in our investments. And just like when you spend someone else's money -- each of us will give and accounting of how we use what God gave us! Are we investing our time to God's glory or to our own destruction.
