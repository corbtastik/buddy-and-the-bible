> **James 1:2-4** My brethren, count it all joy when you fall into various trials, knowing that the testing of your faith produces patience. But let patience have its perfect work, that you may be perfect and complete, lacking nothing.

And old sailor was asked for what purpose rocks and shoals were created, and the reply was, 'That sailor may avoid them.' A philosopher using that saying, when asked for what purpose trials and temptation are sent, answered, 'That we might overcome them.' The truth is we cannot escape the trials and temptations of life, but we can with the help of God use them as food for 'spiritual growth.'

> **James 1:12** Blessed is the man who endures temptation; for when he has been approved, he will receive the crown of life which the Lord has promised to those who love Him.
