**From the Hospital Bed -- Nurses**

William Shakespeare wrote "The meaning of life is to find your gift. The purpose of life is to give it away..."

They say that the American society rest on the backbone of the trucking industry. I say that the medical profession rest on the weary backs of nurses. And I think there is a very real kind of parallel between the profession of nursing and confession of Christianity.

- First of all, the nurse works under and for the physician. She is not the physician but the assistant. The physician diagnoses the problem, plans a remedy and instructs the nurse on the 'what' and 'when' of treatment.
- Second, the nurse is aware of the proper procedures required for the patient to receive the prescribed medication and treatment the physician directs.
- Third, the good nurse demonstrates compassion and sympathy for the patient in answering the constant requests and often complaints from patient and family.

The Parallel is this: All faithful Christians are servants of the Great Physician.

> **Luke 5:31** Jesus answered and said to them, "Those who are well have no need of a physician, but those who are sick. I have not come to call the righteous, but sinners, to repentance."

As Jesus' servants we have the same kind of responsibilities nurses have.

- First of all we serve Jesus: He is the great Physician, He is the one that diagnoses the problem, He is the one that sets the conditions that lead to a cure. We are obligated to follow His instructions, not wavering from truth, not adding to or taking from the gospel. Simply doing "what the Doctor ordered."
- Second, as servants of Jesus we must know what the Great Physician has ordained. Which means we must be about the business of educating ourselves by bible study, prayer, worship and spiritual exercise.
- Third, every Christian must demonstrate compassion and sympathy toward those who are lost. We can't be judgmental, prideful, arrogant or unloving. Far too often people close their minds to the gospel because of the hypocritical attitude and actions of so called Christians.

> **Matthew 11:28-30** "Come to Me, all you who labor and are heavy laden, and I will give you rest. Take My yoke upon you and learn from Me, for I am gentle and lowly in heart, and you will find rest for your souls. For My yoke is easy and My burden is light."
