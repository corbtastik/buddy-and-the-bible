> **Luke 17:3-4** Be on your guard! If your brother sins, rebuke him; and if he repents, forgive him. And if he sins against you seven times a day, and returns to you seven times, saying, 'I repent,' forgive him.

Someone once said the most difficult words in any language to say are "I was wrong, I'm sorry." How do we react when those words are said to us? Sometimes it is just as difficult to respond with mercy as it is to admit one's fault, but that's what Jesus calls on us to do. Remember the unmerciful will never receive mercy from God.

> **Matthew 5:7** Blessed are the merciful, For they shall obtain mercy.

> **Matthew 6:14** For if you forgive men their trespasses, your heavenly Father will also forgive you. But if you do not forgive men their trespasses, neither will your Father forgive your trespasses.
