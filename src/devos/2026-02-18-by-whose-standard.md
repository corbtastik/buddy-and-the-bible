> **Matthew 23:2-4** "The scribes and the Pharisees sit in Moses' seat. Therefore whatever they tell you to observe, that observe and do, but do not do according to their works; for they say, and do not do. For they bind heavy burdens, hard to bear, and lay them on men's shoulders; but they themselves will not move them with one of their fingers."

The self-righteous Pharisees felt it was important if not imperative that others obey the Law to the letter, but not so important that they keep it so carefully. They were judgmental to others but didn't demand the same standard of compliance for themselves.

Sadly enough that same kind of attitude is alive and well among the people who have named the name of Jesus and claim to be God's children. Oh yes, they believe that baptism is necessary for salvation. No, we can't have musical instruments in worship. Yes, we must meet each and every Lord's day and worship! But when principles are taught that apply to personal lives and attitudes it's always "that applies to someone else, not me!" We become quite adamant when someone else does wrong especially if his or her actions have some direct effect on us. But when we do exactly the same thing we feel no guilt or remorse. By whose standard are we living? Certainly not God's.
