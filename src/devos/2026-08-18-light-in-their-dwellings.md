> **Exodus 10:22-23** So Moses stretched out his hand toward heaven, and there was thick darkness in all the land of Egypt three days. They did not see one another; nor did anyone rise from his place for three days. But all the children of Israel had light in their dwellings.

Isn't the plague of darkness a good illustration for the lives of God's children today? We are surrounded by darkness, the darkness of sin and spiritual ignorance. But there is light for God's children.

> **John 8:12** Then Jesus spoke to them again, saying, "I am the light of the world. He who follows Me shall not walk in darkness, but have the light of life."

We have Jesus, and His light guides us through the darkness of a sin-filled, sin-cursed world. And when Jesus is our light, we too become lights those in darkness can see and be drawn to so they can, through faith and obedience, walk in His light as well. "*You are the light of the world. Let your light so shine before men, that they may see your good works and glorify your Father in heaven.*" So don't be a "light hider or a light dimmer" let your light shine bright, by imitating Jesus, in your thoughts, words and deeds.
