> **Titus 2:9-10** Exhort bondservants to be obedient to their own masters, to be well pleasing in all things, not answering back, not pilfering, but showing all good fidelity, that they may adorn the doctrine of God our Savior in all things.

The truth of the matter is God is not pleased with someone just keeping up with the status quo. People who just get by do not reflect in their lives a Christlike attitude or behavior. God's people are those who are always seeking to do more than we have to. We must be working for our bosses as if we are working for God, because indeed we are. You see Christianity is not just about coming to worship, reading your Bible occasionally and praying regularly. It's about being a good worker and striving to better oneself in every endeavor of life. Doing more than you think is expected of you.

> **Ephesians 6:5-8** Bondservants, be obedient to those who are your masters according to the flesh, with fear and trembling, in sincerity of heart, as to Christ; with goodwill doing service, as to the Lord, and not to men, knowing that whatever good anyone does, he will receive the same from the Lord, whether he is a slave or free.
