> **Hebrews 13:8** Jesus Christ is the same yesterday, today, and forever.

There is nothing that gives comfort and peace of mind or security to mankind than knowing that things are going to be and are the same. All doctors agree that babies who are kept on a good schedule, waking up, eating and sleeping at the same time of each day, are more secure and happy babies. Even children and adolescents are more secure and comfortable when they have a schedule at home. We feel secure about our money when we know the bank we put it into is going to be solvent tomorrow. Security in relationships comes from knowing that the feeling each have toward the other is and is going to be the same. So, when Jesus through the Holy Spirit tells us of His perpetual nature, he speaks to one of man's most fundamental needs and greatest sources of comfort and security.

Phrases like "this is the new millennium" or "this is the 21st century" are statements that describe man's attitude about the changing nature of almost everything from marriage to morality and certainly religion. In fact, in the opening remarks of the Baptist manual the men who wrote that book and prescribed Baptist doctrine admitted their teaching differed from N.T. doctrine but justified the change with the simple phrase "things are different now!" But Jesus has not changed. His character is the same and will be the same. His requirements for mercy and salvation are the same and will remain the same. His promises are the same and will not change.

The responsibility that falls on us is to accept the unchanging nature of Jesus and follow His unchanging law. Then His unchanging promise of eternal life, He will keep for all who "obey Him."
