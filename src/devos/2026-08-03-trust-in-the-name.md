> **Psalm 20:6-7** Now I know that the LORD saves His anointed; He will answer him from His holy heaven with the saving strength of His right hand. Some trust in chariots, and some in horses; but we will remember the name of the LORD our God.

The reality is we tend to put our trust in things we can see, rather than things we can't see. Some trust in military might for protection. Some put their faith in the political system of our nation to shield them. Far too many trust themselves, their own intelligence or physical strength. Still others fill their lives with pleasure and fun to give them purpose in living. When this is true, they are walking by sight rather than by faith. The Psalmist understood the power, wisdom and providence of God. His walk was a walk of faith, because his trust was in the name of the Lord our God.

> **2 Corinthians 5:7** For we walk by faith, not by sight. **9-10** Therefore we make it our aim, whether present or absent, to be well pleasing to Him. For we must all appear before the judgment seat of Christ, that each one may receive the things done in the body, according to what he has done, whether good or bad.
