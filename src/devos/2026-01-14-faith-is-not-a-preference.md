scripture: "Romans 10:17"
summary: "Faith is not based upon I like or what I prefer, but upon what God says."
---
> **Romans 10:17** So then faith *comes* by hearing, and hearing by the word of God.

> **2 Corinthians 5:7** For we walk by faith, not by sight.

In the Scriptures one hears God's truth and is convicted, even if such runs against preconceived ideas or personal preferences. Faith is not based upon "I like" or what "I prefer" but upon, "what God says" In the book of Acts, New Testament Christians came from all different backgrounds and religions because they were convicted by God's truth and could not deny it, and they were persuaded by the overwhelming evidence. Yet more and more people are viewing faith as believing in something "they like" or what "sounds good to them". Since their beliefs will tend to have no foundation other than their preferences and personality, they will interpret any criticism of their beliefs as a personal attack. Since 'everyone has the right to their own opinion', they do not mind if you do not agree with them, but they will become defensive and sometimes angry if you try to change their opinions. Thus, Christians will be surprised when people fly off the handle when their point of view is challenged. One writer says, "Today religion is not seen as a set of beliefs about what is real and what is not. Rather, religion is seen as a preference, a choice. We believe in what we like."

> **Matthew 15:8-9** 'These people draw near to Me with their mouth, and honor Me with *their* lips, but their heart is far from Me. And in vain they worship Me, teaching *as* doctrines the commandments of men.'
