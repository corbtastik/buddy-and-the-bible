> **Matthew 7:24-27** "Therefore whoever hears these sayings of Mine, and does them, I will liken him to a wise man who built his house on the rock: and the rain descended, the floods came, and the winds blew and beat on that house; and it did not fall, for it was founded on the rock. But everyone who hears these sayings of Mine, and does not do them, will be like a foolish man who built his house on the sand: and the rain descended, the floods came, and the winds blew and beat on that house; and it fell. And great was its fall."

It's not difficult to understand how a house built on sand will not last long, while a house built on a solid foundation will be able to stand up to the worst of storms -- but what is the real message Jesus wants us to get out of this short story? Basically, the only difference between the wise man and the foolish man is that one put Jesus' words into practice, while the other did not put his words into practice. A wise person will build his or her life on the teachings of Jesus, while a foolish person will ignore the teachings he or she received, and live life as they please. When the storms (trials and tribulations) come along, the life that has a solid foundation will be able to persevere and stand strong, while the life that does not have a solid foundation will quickly crumble and fall apart.

Are you building your life on a solid foundation? Are you building your life on the teachings of Jesus, or are you working off of a different set of blueprints? Time will eventually put your foundation to the test. If you are not building your life on the teachings of Jesus, your life may eventually fall with a great crash but most certainly your eternal destiny will be eternal destruction.
