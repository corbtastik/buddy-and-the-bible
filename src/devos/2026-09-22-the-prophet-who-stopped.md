> **1 Kings 13:8-10** But the man of God said to the king, "If you were to give me half your house, I would not go in with you; nor would I eat bread nor drink water in this place. For so it was commanded me by the word of the LORD, saying, 'You shall not eat bread, nor drink water, nor return by the same way you came.'" So he went another way and did not return by the way he came to Bethel.

What a bold, courageous and obedient thing to say to the king. But the young prophet, sent by God was determined to accomplish the work God sent him to do. Condemn Jeroboam's "new" religion. There are so many positive things about this young man: He was young but still chosen; when called he went before "The King" - one who had the power of "life & death"; he spoke God's word -- even though it was unpopular and dangerous; he shows pity - a good attitude, different than Jonah's. he refused the king's reward - not interested in "material goods" like Elisha's servant or Balaam; and then he started back as God commanded.

But then he Stopped -- nothing said about God commanding him to not stop and rest, but the young man failed to understand the urgent need to "get out of Dodge." He was in enemy territory he needed to get back to Judah. Because when he stopped the devil caught up with him. He allowed himself to be distracted from his purpose and God's commands. The Young Prophet got off to a good start, but when he stopped, then failed to ask God for instructions. We can learn from this. We must be zealous to continue our journey the same way we began it. Don't stop and don't allow the devil to distract you from your goal of heaven. Always keep God's instruction at the front of our mind.
