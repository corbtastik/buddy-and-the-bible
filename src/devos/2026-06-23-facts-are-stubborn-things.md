> **2 Timothy 2:15** "Be diligent to present yourself approved to God as a worker who does not need to be ashamed, accurately handling the word of truth."

Brother Matt shared this quote with me from John Adams: "Facts are stubborn things; and whatever may be our wishes, our inclination, ore the dictates of our passions, they cannot alter the sate of facts and evidence."

Roger Shouse in his morning article pointed to a woman "pastor" who actually used the miraculous raising of Lazarus and an illustration that God accepts the LGBT choices made by some in our nation today. It is interesting, as sad as it is, that people use something as beautiful as God's word to justify something as horrible as sin. But the devil's been swaying man to do that since the Garden of Eden and man continues to look for ways to assuage his conscience so he can do anything and everything he desires. But no matter how man might twist the truth he does nothing but creates a lie, man hasn't the ability to alter what is true. Facts are facts regardless of one's perception, or inclination. Truth never changes and in the end, truth will be the judge of all mankind.

> **2 Peter 3:15b-16** As also our beloved brother Paul, according to the wisdom given to him, has written to you, as also in all his epistles, speaking in them of these things, in which are some things hard to understand, which untaught and unstable people twist to their own destruction, as they do also the rest of the Scriptures.
