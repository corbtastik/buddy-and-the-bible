---
> **Hebrews 6:9-12** But, beloved, we are confident of better things concerning you, yes, things that accompany salvation, though we speak in this manner. For God is not unjust to forget your work and labor of love which you have shown toward His name, in that you have ministered to the saints, and do minister. And we desire that each one of you show the same diligence to the full assurance of hope until the end, that you do not become sluggish, but imitate those who through faith and patience inherit the promises.

The writer is contrasting those who fall away who crucify afresh the Son of God by their unfaithfulness. His admonition is to do the things that accompany salvation which include diligence until the end. Their assurance was that God would not forget their labor. If they did not to become sluggish or lazy! But follow the example of other faithful people who have shown us that God requires our constant faithful endeavors as long as we live.

Someone told me that this wasn't very comforting to them. But comfort is not found in the fact that we must work hard alone! That would be discouraging. The comfort comes from: Knowing that with God's help we can remain faithful (it ain't easy but it ain't impossible). We don't have to do it alone, in fact we cannot do it alone. We need God's help and He is willing to provide it abundantly.

> **Philippians 4:13** I can do all things through Christ who strengthens me.
