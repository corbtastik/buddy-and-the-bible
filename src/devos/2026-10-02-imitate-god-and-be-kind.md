> **Matthew 5:38-42** You have heard that it was said, 'an eye for an eye and a tooth for a tooth.' But I tell you not to resist an evil person. But whoever slaps you on your right cheek, turn the other to him also. If anyone wants to sue you and take away your tunic, let him have your cloak also. And whoever compels you to go one mile, go with him two. Give to him who asks you, and from him who wants to borrow from you do not turn away."

Oh, how much trouble could we avoid if only we would be kind to one another! Kindness is an attribute of love that flows out of a compassionate heart. Be kind to others by imparting goodness with your words and your actions. Be kind, even when they are not kind to you. Remember, kindness forgives others like God in Christ forgives us. Overcome evil by doing good toward those who wrong you. They won't be expecting it! Imitate God and be kind.

> **Ephesians 4:32** And be kind to one another, tenderhearted, forgiving one another, even as God in Christ forgave you.

> **2 Peter 1:5-7** But also for this very reason, giving all diligence, add to your faith virtue, to virtue knowledge, to knowledge self-control, to self-control perseverance, to perseverance godliness, to godliness brotherly kindness, and to brotherly kindness love.
