> *"Let all those who seek you rejoice and be glad in You.., let the hearts of those rejoice who seek the Lord.., rejoice in the Lord, you righteous, and give thanks.., shout joyfully to the Lord all the earth; break forth in song, rejoice, and sing praise.., be glad and rejoice with me.., rejoice in the Lord always, again I say rejoice.., rejoice always, pray without ceasing, in everything give thanks."*

What lessons do you learn from these excerpts of scripture? Do you think God wants His people to be happy? There can be no doubt, that God expects us to rejoice and be happy in the lives each of us lives. Why then are the vast majority of Americans including Christians not happy? How do we explain that so many Christians cannot find positive things in their lives to rejoice about, when the bible clearly teaches in 100s of passages that God wants his people to be happy? It seems to me that there is only ONE basic hindrance to the happiness of the children of God and that is they don't want to be. It was Abraham Lincoln who observed, *"People are about as happy as they make up their minds to be."* Centuries before honest Abe, Roman Emperor Marcus Antonius said, *"No man is happy who does not think himself so."* Is it not clear if our Creator tells us to be happy, that happiness is possible, even while living in a sin cursed world? True happiness can only be found in having a saved relationship with Jesus. He has left His joy for His disciples to experience and hope in. So, *Rejoice in the Lord always; again, I will say, rejoice!*

> *John 15:10-11 If you keep My commandments, you will abide in My love, just as I have kept My Father's commandments and abide in His love. These things I have spoken to you, that My joy may remain in you, and that your joy may be full.*
