> *James 1:23-24 For if anyone is a hearer of the word and not a doer, he is like a man observing his natural face in a mirror; for he observes himself, goes away, and immediately forgets what kind of man he was.*

James is talking about self-examination. Are we honestly examining our thoughts, our words and our actions to see if we are like our Lord or if we are we like the rest of the world? But just as important we need to look into the mirror and realize what other people see in me as I live from day to day. What do people see in my life? Do they see someone interested only in self? Someone willing to compromise convictions just to be like those around me? Someone who talks a lot about being a Christian but lives a completely different life? What we see in the mirror about ourselves others see as well. We can't hide our true self from the rest of the world. We may try but the truth comes out sooner or later -- so we need to be careful and diligent in our efforts to be LIGHT AND SALT helping others to come to Christ instead of leading them to destruction.

> *Matthew 5:14-16 You are the light of the world. A city that is set on a hill cannot be hidden. Nor do they light a lamp and put it under a basket, but on a lampstand, and it gives light to all [who are] in the house. Let your light so shine before men, that they may see your good works and glorify your Father in heaven.*
