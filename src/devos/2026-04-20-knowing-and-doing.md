> *James 4:17 Therefore, to one who knows the right thing to do and does not do it, to him it is sin.*

> *Proverbs 3:27-28 Do not withhold good from those to whom it is due, when it is in your power to do it. Do not say to your neighbor, "Go, and come back, and tomorrow I will give it," when you have it with you.*

One of the important lessons from the book of James is "knowing and doing are two separate acts." While it is important to know the right thing to do, but not doing it is missing the mark. One needs to grasp the concept that knowledge needs to be applied to be worth anything. The Christian life is brought about by the new birth, but that new birth must bring about a new way of thinking and living. It means conducting one's life according to the pattern revealed to us in the scriptures. And the pattern reveals repeatedly that we are to be about the business of applying our knowledge as opportunity presents itself. It is beyond our ability to know if or when an opportunity might present itself again. We cannot think that the good someone needs today, will have to wait until tomorrow. You may not have the opportunity tomorrow to say that word that might lead an erring brother or sister back to the Lord. You may not have the opportunity tomorrow to share the gospel with a lost friend or relative. You may not have the opportunity tomorrow to do a good deed that will help and encourage someone else.

You cannot do a worthwhile deed too soon, because you never know how soon it will be too late. Live today in such a way that you will not regret what you left undone.
